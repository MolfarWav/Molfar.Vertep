Molfar page, variant C: "Studio, three panes"

Static mockup, 2026-10-11. Dark first; the toggle switches the warm light theme.

The idea

Working on an app with Molfar should feel like a small studio: projects on the left, the conversation in the middle, the project itself on the right. The pane is the project page in compact form, so instructions, memory, skills and checkpoints are always one glance away while you talk. Expand the pane and it becomes the full project page (frame 2); the same content, no second place to learn.
  • Left: a rail of projects (icon, name, app/free badge) plus the chat list of the selected project; the "No project" entry holds loose chats.
  • Header: breadcrumb on the left; a live strip on the right: model, reasoning, context meter, cost, inspector, connection as a dot. The red banner became a dismissible toast, bottom-right.
  • Composer: as wide as the centre column, quick actions above it while it is empty, model / reasoning / context / compact in its footer. Collapse the pane and it grows to about 1000 px.
  • Phone: one pane at a time, a Chat | Project segmented control under the header, projects and chats in a drawer (bonus frame 5), composer on the bottom, project cards as accordions with a sticky "Save checkpoint" bar.
