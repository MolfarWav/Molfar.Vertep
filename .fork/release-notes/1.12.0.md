# 🧠 Molfar Vertep 1.12.0

No engine code changed. **Roleplay 4.100.0 arrives as an app update**: a long story of many chats keeps its beginning, and the stories turn on remembered events.

## 📚 A long story keeps its beginning
- The backstory used to read only the three nearest earlier chats; in chat 5 the first chat dropped out without a word. Now every older chat of the chain (up to 40 back) comes in as **one condensed part** placed before the near ones: its turning points, its pinned and key facts and one line for each arc. No model call. It takes a share of the Backstory budget (Library > Litopys settings > "In the prompt" > "Share for the far chats", 18% by default, 5 to 40); when it is short the oldest chats are cut first.
- It shows as its own source: a row in "What this chat carries", a fold in What fired (last reply) and a count for the whole chat.
- A Data Bank **book** saved for an earlier chat now reaches every later chat of the chain, not only the next three. Card memory still reads three.

## 📍 Turning points
- Events the story turns on (a betrayal, a death, a vow broken) are kept nearly word for word in their own list, not among facts, with one line for how each character they touch feels about it. The chapter worker reports at most one per scene; a scene you mark always gets one; a Dashboard line of strength 3 is shown to it as a candidate. A later one may undo an earlier one or change a character's feeling; a change against what you wrote waits for your yes.
- Only people who know one get it (as with facts). One that retells a lie told in the scene is marked **untrue**: those who believe it read it as plain, everyone else is told it is untrue; "Not a lie" in its menu.
- Library > Litopys > Ledger has a new tab **Turning points** (add, mark a chapter, edit, undo, remove, answer what is waiting). In Activity, a turning point the worker found has an **Undo**. "Turning points shown" (12 by default, 3 to 30) is next to the far share in the settings.

## 🖥️ Two screens made easier
- The **lorebook entry panel** opens from the left over the list and the book, almost the whole width (the Library drawer goes full screen while it is open); the text is on the left, tall, and when it appears, keys and folder on the right. A phone is unchanged.
- **World settings** look like the Litopys settings: folding colour blocks with one summary line, switches instead of a checkbox.

## Known and left
- The worker's new rule was not tried on a real model; small free models may return few or odd turning points.
- The "untrue" mark is a word-overlap guess; a lie exposed later does not change a turning point by itself.
- At the default Backstory budget (800) a chain of four or more chats has little room left for the oldest near chat; raise the budget for a long story.
- Next: deep memory over the archived chats of the chain (Molfar.Vertep#264).

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart, then Update on Roleplay in Updates. `dev` only updates the engine, not the Roleplay app. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page.
