# 🩹 Molfar Vertep 1.0.6

A small release of fixes from Discord reports: Update now works on Windows 10 again, an edited card no longer breaks Roleplay, menus scroll on Android again, and there is now a button to report a bug.

### 🐞 Report a bug from the app
**Report a bug** sits in the account menu (also inside apps) and at the bottom of the Apps page. Describe what happened and add your device and browser (guessed for you, change it if it is wrong). On a computer, **Take a screenshot** saves a picture to your downloads; on a phone, take one the usual way. The report also carries the versions, screen size, language and the last errors the app caught.

Then press **Open on GitHub** for a pre-filled issue (a free GitHub account is needed), or **Copy for Discord** and paste it in our Discord. Nothing is sent from the app by itself: you see the whole text first.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Windows: Update now failed** with a "Can't set time" warning for every file. Molfar Vertep now unpacks the update itself instead of using Windows' tar. If your copy is 1.0.5 or older and the update failed, download 1.0.6 below once by hand; later updates work from the app.
- **Roleplay 4.41.1: "Script error" after a card edit.** A card edited by Molfar, a script or by hand could take the whole app down when its editor opened. Lists written as one line, numbers where text goes and similar slips are now read the way the app expects. If a page still fails to draw, it says what went wrong, with **Copy details** and **Try again**, and the rest of the app keeps working.
- **Roleplay 4.41.1: menus did not scroll on Android.** On Chrome and Brave for Android the lower part of a sheet or menu (the chat menu, Dashboard settings) could not be scrolled while a character's expression sprite was on screen under it. The sprite now steps aside while a menu is open.
- Errors inside apps in live editing now show their real message instead of a bare "Script error.".
- Molfar can undo its own edit of an app's data file (a card, a lorebook, settings): a checkpoint restore now offers those files one by one, after you confirm. Chats and saves written since stay.

## Other changes
- The GitHub bug report form is rewritten for Molfar Vertep and links our Discord for questions.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay fix comes with the app 4.41.1:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. It works on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.6".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.6`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.6-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.6-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.6` or `:latest`.
