# 🧾 Molfar Vertep 1.3.5

Roleplay's story threads become quests. This release is Roleplay 4.65.0, which arrives as an app update; the engine itself has no code change.

### 🧭 Quests instead of threads
The threads on the Dashboard are now **quests**: **main** (what the story is about right now), **side** (an optional line the story can drop) and **everyday** (a small errand of the moment that ends by itself after a few turns). A main quest can have **steps**, and shows "2 of 4 steps done". Limits: one main quest, three side quests, four steps per main quest; all of them are settings in Dashboard settings.

Nothing is lost in silence any more. When the story proposes a quest and its kind is full, the quest waits under **Suggestions**: **Add it** (it asks which open quest it replaces) or **Not now**. When a quest has stayed open through three checks and nothing moved, it comes up there too: **Close it** or **Keep open**. Everyday quests are the only ones closed without your word. A quest you add yourself at a full limit waits there as well instead of failing. Closing a main quest closes its open steps with it; reopening it brings them back.

The model that writes the replies sees main quests first with their steps, then side quests. Everyday ones stay on the Dashboard and are not sent.

### 🌪 Story, move: pick a quest or a step, or add a Twist
The menu next to Send lists the quests with their steps under them. Pick one, and the next reply pulls on it. The new **Twist** item makes the next reply let something happen that belongs to none of the open quests, built from what is already in the scene. It has its own prompt in Dashboard settings, with Restore.

### 🔎 Event ids the sensor makes up (#86)
Small models invent event names (`jealousy`, `check_on_her`). A close name now counts as the known event (`jealous_scene`, `checked_on_her`); a name nobody can place is counted across chats and listed in Dashboard settings, **Event vocabulary**, under **Not recognised**, where you can **Add as event** or **Ignore** it. A "significant" event that comes with no quote from the text now counts as a routine one, and the Dashboard says how many.

### Also
- The sensor prompt changed (it asks for a kind and steps); a stored copy of the previous default follows the update. A copy you changed stays as it is.
- Threads, steps and the Story, move texts say **quests** everywhere, in English and Ukrainian.

## Get it
Run from source with `Molfar-Vertep.bat` (latest release tag), or download the archive for your system from the assets below. The quests arrive as an app update through the Updates panel.
