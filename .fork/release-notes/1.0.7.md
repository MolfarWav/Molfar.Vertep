# 🩹 Molfar Vertep 1.0.7

The first Tuesday bug batch: no more lost drafts, full-length replies on Chub, texting scenes the model remembers, and replies that survive a reload. Most of it comes with the Roleplay app 4.43.0.

### ✍️ Your draft survives a reload
The message you were typing could vanish mid-sentence when the app reloaded itself: when a fresh build of the app landed, or the engine restarted with newer code. The app kept the draft, but its storage started over from the moment the app was opened. Now the draft is still there after any reload.

### 📱 Texting scenes stay texting scenes (Roleplay 4.43.0)
When you text, call or write to a character, the Dashboard used to leave them out of the scene, because they were not physically there. The model then got no scene notes at all and soon wrote as if you were face to face. Now such a character stays in the scene, and the notes say you are not in the same place and how you talk: by text, on a call, on a video call or by letters. When you meet in person, it switches back.

### 🔄 A reply survives a reload (Roleplay 4.43.0, needs engine 1.0.7)
Reload the page while a reply is being written, and the chat now shows "Writing" with the live text and picks up the reply when it is saved. Before, the reply looked lost and popped up later between other messages. If a reply never arrives (the model failed, the server restarted), "Writing" now ends with an error instead of counting forever.

### 🎲 Dice say what they decide (Roleplay 4.43.0)
The dice tool now takes what the roll is for and an optional difficulty. The result reads "Leap over the gap: 1d20 → 14 vs 12, success", in the model's notes and on the chip in the chat. If you never edited the dice tool, it picks this up by itself.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Replies cut at about 500 tokens on Chub.** Custom OpenAI-compatible connections now send the output limit the way every compatible server reads it (`max_tokens`). Chub's endpoints ignored the old field and stopped every reply at their own default.
- **Roleplay 4.43.0: a long draft was squeezed between the buttons.** Once your draft wraps, the box takes the whole width and the buttons move under it, on phones and on desktop.
- **Roleplay 4.43.0: gallery images vanished after adding several.** Images now go to the engine's asset store and the card keeps only their link, so a big gallery saves. Older images move there the next time you add one. If a character save fails, the change is undone on screen and the message says why, instead of "connection lost".

## Other changes
- Apps can ask whether a chat's reply is still being written (`GET /v1/apps/<app>/__generating?chatId=`).
- One app request may carry 8 MB (was 1 MB).
- Error texts in Roleplay say Molfar Vertep, not Chrysalis.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.43.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. The reply pick-up after a reload needs engine 1.0.7; everything else works on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.7".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.7`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.7-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.7-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.7` or `:latest`.
