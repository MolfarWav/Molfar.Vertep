# 🧠 Molfar Vertep 1.12.1

No engine code changed. **Roleplay 4.102.0 arrives as an app update**: a story that goes on over several chats can now bring back the exact words of an earlier chat.

## 🔎 Deep memory over the archived chats of a story
- When a chat continues another, the original messages of the earlier chats of the same story (up to 40 back) are searched by their words. When what is being said shares at least two rare words with an old passage, one or two short word-for-word passages come into the reply. Facts and summaries kept the gist; this brings back the line itself (a name, an oath, a number).
- **On by default.** It only changes a chat that continues another one, and only when the match is strong, so most replies are unchanged. It costs no model call. Switch: Library > Litopys settings > "In the prompt" > "Word-for-word memories of earlier chats".
- **The index is made when the earlier chat goes to the Archive** (also when you press "Continue in a new chat"), so the first reply of the new chat is not slowed. A chat archived before this version is indexed in the background, one chat at a time.
- **Only the same story.** The search goes along the chain of chats that continue one another, not across your other stories.
- **What fired** now counts it over the whole chat: Litopys tab, Whole chat, "Earlier chats, word for word", and the Memory view shows how many rare words each passage shares with the scene.

## 🧪 Old chapters by meaning (experimental, off)
- A new switch, "Also find old chapters by meaning" in the same place, lets the embeddings of your chapters (the ones already made for the Vectors tab) point to an old chapter when it lies unusually far above all the others. Meaning only picks the chapter; a passage still comes in only if it also shares a rare word with the scene, so meaning alone never adds text.
- It needs an embeddings model and a story of at least about 17 chapters; a short story opens nothing. It is off because it has not been calibrated on a real long story yet (Molfar.Vertep#300). Leave it off unless you want to help test it.

## Known and left
- Tried on made-up long chats (a planted detail found 10 times out of 10, an ordinary scan brought a passage in about 1 of 120); not yet on a real chain of long chats.
- The guide's translations of five pages (Canvas, Litopys, Long chats, Memory, World DM) are not refreshed yet in the 13 other languages; the reader shows the English page.
- Next: calibrate the meaning switch on a real long story (#300).

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart, then Update on Roleplay in Updates. `dev` only updates the engine, not the Roleplay app. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page.
