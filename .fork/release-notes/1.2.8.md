# ⚡ Molfar Vertep 1.2.8

A small fix release: the prompt cache works again, and Vertep Lite's plan stays out of the story. Both come with Roleplay 4.57.0, an app update.

### 💾 The prompt cache works again
A provider keeps only the unchanged start of a request in its cache. The Dashboard note and the Litopys facts change every reply, and they sat above the chat history, so the provider read the whole history again at full price on every reply, whatever the preset. They now sit just before your newest message. The last Litopys chapter stays above the history, because it changes only when a chapter closes. Over 40 turns, 78% of the prompt came from the cache instead of 62%.

In **Vertep Lite** the lorebook entries also move below the history, so an entry that turns on or off no longer costs the whole history.

How long a provider keeps its cache is up to the provider. On NanoGPT, Gemma 4 31B kept it for 3 to 6 minutes in our test, and GLM 5.3 cached only some of the time. A slow reply with long thinking, plus the time you spend reading, can outlast it.

### 🔍 See the cache in chat info
The **i** in a chat's header has a new line, **Cached, last reply**: how much of the last prompt came from the cache, for example "6144 of 8844 (69%)". Its tooltip has the total for the chat. The **Context** line now counts the cached part too; before, a chat with a good cache looked emptier than it was.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Vertep Lite's plan in the story.** Models that think on their own (GLM, Gemma) wrote the plan in `<plan>` tags, and it showed up as a numbered list above the reply. It now goes into the reply's thinking.

## Other changes
- pi stays at 1.2.0: 1.3.0 is younger than the 7 days a new pi version waits.
