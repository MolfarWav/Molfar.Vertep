# 🏠 Molfar Vertep 1.16.1

A small release about matching by meaning (embeddings) and about the prompts of the older presets. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then press Update on **Roleplay** (4.112.0) in Updates.

## 🧠 One model, two names, and vectors that stay on each device (#374)
The same model goes by different names: on this computer it is `bge-m3`, on OpenRouter `baai/bge-m3`. Every stored vector carries the name of the model that made it, and a vector of another name is made again. So a laptop on the local model and a phone on OpenRouter's rewrote each other's vector files after every sync: repeated embedding calls, a delay, and no matching by meaning until the vectors were rebuilt.
- **The engine now knows the aliases.** `bge-m3` (with its OpenRouter, Hugging Face and Ollama names) and OpenAI's three embedding models are each one model under any name. Different models stay different. Switching one device between the local bge-m3 and OpenRouter's bge-m3 keeps its vectors.
- **The vectors stay on the device that made them (Roleplay 4.112.0).** The chat vectors of Litopys, the lorebook vectors, the Data Bank vectors and the "does embedding work here" status moved out of the synced folders into the app's local cache. A computer and a phone can now use different models without touching each other's vectors. Chapters, facts, chats and everything else of the memory are still synced.
- **Settings > Memory** says so under the model field, and so does the guide.

## 🎭 Roleplay 4.112.0: the presets explain the app's notes
Vertep Lite told the model what the notes in square brackets are (the story record, the relationship dashboard, world news and events, your directions); FRANKENX and Molfar did not, or only in part. Now all three do, in the style of each preset. Vertep Lite also lists the stage directions note (`[[...]]`) it was missing. A built-in copy takes the new text on update; a preset you copied and edited keeps its own text.

## Known limits
- The vectors are made again on a device that has none: by itself (**Make vectors by itself**) for chats and lorebooks, and with the button for the Data Bank. The first update on a synced pair moves each device's old files over; a device that is not updated yet loses its vectors once and makes them again.
- Not measured: how close the vectors of the local int8 bge-m3 and the online full-precision one are. It matters only when one device switches between them.
- Not tried on a real pair of synced devices, and the new hint in Settings was not looked at in a browser. The preset text was not tried on a model.
- The new guide strings are translated by a cheap model without a native read.

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page. Apps: Updates > Roleplay > Update. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
