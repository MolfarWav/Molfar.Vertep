# 💞 Molfar Vertep 0.8.0

The full relationship dashboard comes to Roleplay: it follows every reply as it lands, you can tune everything it does, and the notebook and story threads are yours to edit. Your apps, chats and settings are kept.

## ✨ What's new

### ⚡ Roleplay 4.23.0: the dashboard keeps up with every reply (through app updates)
Open **Updates** in the top bar to install Roleplay 4.23.0. The dashboard now reads a reply right after it lands instead of about a minute later. Edit the newest message or press **Continue** and it is read again; a new swipe gets its own state, and switching back brings the old one. A small line under the newest message tells you what is going on: updating, updated (how long ago, how many seconds the reading took, and what the characters just learned), behind, or not updated with a **Retry**. The sensor line under the strip shows the same by colour. In manual mode nothing runs until you press **Refresh**.

### ⚙️ Dashboard settings
A gear in the wide view (and in the phone sheet) opens the dashboard's own settings:
- **Sensor:** automatic or manual, which model reads the turns, how long its reply may be, the prompt insert and how many tokens it may take, catch-up, automatic souls, which groups of events count, and counters of calls, tokens and the last reading time.
- **Event vocabulary:** change how much an event moves each attitude, switch events off, add your own, or reset to the defaults.
- **What the model sees:** the exact words a character gets before a reply, their size against the limit (which you can change right there), and three checks: no numbers, no blind spot, only that character's notebook.
- **Tune with Molfar:** describe what you want changed and Molfar opens with an unsent request to edit the vocabulary or settings for you.
- **The sensor prompt in blocks:** role, language, truth, events, scene, knowledge, threads and reply size, each edited and restored on its own.

### 📓 Your notebook and your threads
Every note in a character's notebook can be edited or removed (removed notes can be restored), and you can write your own, marked "written by you". Mark notes **pinned** (always reaches the story model), **important** (goes first) or **everyday** (dropped first when the words do not fit). Story threads can be resolved, reopened, edited or added by hand, you choose how many may be open at once, and every few turns the sensor is asked whether old threads are settled. A greeting or a question with nothing at stake is no longer filed as a thread.

### 🗣️ How characters address you
The dashboard notes what each character calls you: your name, a nickname, a title, a pet name. A change shows in the character's story ("Now calls you «…»"), and the story model uses it. A character who plainly knows you, such as an adviser who serves you, counts as knowing your name even if it was never spoken in the chat.

### 🌗 Easier to read
Each part of the day has its own colour and sign. The constellation spreads small values so a change of a few points is visible, with a legend that says how to read it. The notebook chips in the strip open the notebook. Long boxes in the wide view scroll inside instead of stretching the page.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Start values from a Soul saved later now apply.** A character who entered the story before their Soul had start values (or whose start values you changed afterwards) shifts by the difference, and the story list says so.
- **Characters no longer drop out of the scene** when a cheap sensor model forgets them while they act or speak.
- **Shorter, cleaner sensor replies:** no blind spot or notes about your own character, no single summary handed to everyone as a fact, and each character's pronouns from their Soul, so notes stop getting a character's gender wrong. Replies that still run long can be given a higher limit in the settings.

## Other changes
- Self-update also recognises release archives named `Molfar-Vertep-*`. File names stay `Chrysalis-*` in this release; a later release can rename them without stranding copies from 0.8.0 on.
- The README tells the project's story first, with the technical details below.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.8.0". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.23.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.8.0`.
- **Downloads** below: `Chrysalis-0.8.0-<system>` (file names stay as before so self-update keeps working). The Android APK is debug-signed and cannot update an installed copy.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.8.0` or `:latest`.
