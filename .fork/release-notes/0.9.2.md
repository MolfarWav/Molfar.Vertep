# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.9.2

Models get one home. Which models you see, your quick switch, and every model's context, prices and parameters now live in one Settings tab, and they work the same for Roleplay, Molfar and every app. Roleplay 4.27.0 adds chats that continue each other and portraits everywhere.

## ✨ What's new

### 🧭 Connections and models, one tab
**Settings → Connections and models** replaces the two tabs "API connections" and "Models":
- **Quick switch** on top: star the models you switch between, give them names of your own ("Fast one", "Writer"), put them in order. Roleplay's chat and Molfar offer them first.
- **One search** over every model of every connection.
- Under each connection, its models: a box to **show it in the pickers**, the **star**, and the **model's settings**.

### 🎛️ Parameters belong to the model
Open a model's settings to set its **context window**, its **prices**, and its **parameters**, in blocks by who calls the model:
- **Chat**: Roleplay replies and API callers. Temperature, top P and K, min P, penalties, seed, max output, reasoning, thinking tags, custom parameters (JSON) and extra headers.
- **Plugins**: trackers, memory, translation. A short set, usually a low temperature and reasoning off.
- **One plugin** of your choice, for example the relationship dashboard's sensor.
- **Molfar**: its own set.

A field you switch off is not sent. Not sure what to set? Each block has **Recommended settings** in plain words and a button that fills them in. Molfar warns when a model's context window is too small for it.

### 🎭 Roleplay 4.27.0 (through app updates)
- **No more Connections section.** You pick the chat's model from the preset · persona · model switch in the chat (on phones it sits in the bottom bar): your quick switch first, then the other models, and **Set up** opens the model's settings. Each chat keeps its own model. Your old connection profiles become your quick switch, with their names.
- **The model's parameters come first.** Your preset fills in only what the model leaves unset; a switch in the preset lets it win instead. The first time, your default preset's samplers become the current model's parameters, so nothing changes in how your chats sound.
- **Chats that continue each other.** In the Library, the map shows one chat: the chats it continues above, the ones that continue it below. Link an earlier chat and the new one gets the earlier story as backstory in every reply.
- **Portraits everywhere.** A portrait set in the Library shows on the dashboard and in Soul too.
- **Full-screen drawers**, a **"Story, move"** button that only arms the note until you send, and fixes for copying the card id and for prices like "$0.42000000000000004".

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Android:** if the provider list fails to load when you add a connection, you now see the error and **Try again**, instead of a list with only the sign-ins.
- Older presets with the reasoning level "min" no longer switch reasoning off.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.9.2". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.27.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.9.2`.
- **Downloads** below: `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.2-<system>` for desktops and `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.2-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer (installs over 0.9.1 and keeps your data).
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.9.2` or `:latest`.
