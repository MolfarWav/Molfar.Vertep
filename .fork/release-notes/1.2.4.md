# 🧭 Molfar Vertep 1.2.4

Tell Molfar what to change while he is still working, see everything that waits on you in Roleplay in one list, and let a device take over the sync when you don't want what it holds.

### ✍️ Correct Molfar while he works
While Molfar works, the message box keeps Stop and also sends: type a correction or an extra detail and press Enter (or the arrow next to Stop). Molfar reads it at his next step and goes on with it, without starting over. The message stays in the chat where it arrived, and Molfar remembers it in later replies. Pictures and files wait until he finishes.

### 📥 An inbox in Roleplay
Roleplay 4.52.0 has an **Inbox** above Help (on a phone, the first row under More). It lists the chats where the Library suggests changes to the story record and the cards with proposed souls, with the total on its badge. Click a row to decide right there.

### 🧹 Litopys keeps long chats in order
In Library → a chat → Facts, **Consolidate facts** (from 20 facts) looks for duplicates and contradictions and proposes merges, rewrites and retirements. Nothing changes until you accept a proposal. In a long chat, facts written or used lately also come first in the story record, and old unused ones sink slowly.

### 🔁 Replace the sync with this device
A device that never synced, or one that fell behind, could only pull what the sync holds. When you don't want that, Settings → Sync now offers **Replace the sync with this device** under the "Pull first" notice. After a confirmation the sync holds exactly this device's files, and your other devices follow on their next pull.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Custom connections that failed every reply** with "malformed server-sent event JSON" since 1.2.2. Their stream is now read tolerantly: events that are not JSON are skipped and noted in the log.
- **Roleplay previews showing the model's reasoning** (4.52.0): the chat list, Recent chats, the Home quote and the chat panel now hide what the chat itself hides (its display regex and reasoning blocks).
- **Roleplay display regex switching itself off** on slow phones (4.52.0): the first rule of a session could run out of time while the helper started, and then stayed off.

## Other changes
- **Roleplay 4.52.0** (an app update, Apps → Updates): the Molfar preset's section names read plainly ("📜 Directives" instead of "━📜 Directives (PICK ONE)"); the Consolidate failure message says what to do next.

## How to get it
From source: `Molfar-Vertep.bat` picks up the new release. Downloads: the archives and the Android APK on this release page. Roleplay 4.52.0 comes through Apps → Updates. Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
