# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 1.0.3

Small things that were in the way. Five reviewers went through the shell, Molfar's page and the Roleplay app on desktop and on a phone; this release fixes every small item they found. The Roleplay part comes with the app 4.39.0.

### 🔗 The Chats panel opens what it names (Roleplay 4.39.0)
On the Chats page, a member chip opens that character's card, the group's name or portrait opens the group editor, and the chat's persona, preset and each lorebook open in their own section. The links have a dotted underline, so you see them on a phone too. A preset or book that no longer exists stays plain text.

### 🗣️ Errors in plain words (Roleplay 4.39.0)
"Sensor reply was not JSON" is gone. The dashboard says the scene state could not be updated and that it will try again on the next reply; the raw reason sits under **Details**. A failed soul rating and an unreachable marketplace say the same kind of thing.

### 🧭 Fewer "what does this do?" moments
- **Molfar's page:** the mode switch reads **Mode: Full**, and each mode says what it allows: Full plans big changes first, then edits and runs commands; Accept asks before each shell command; Plan only reads and plans. "Apply" under a reply is now **Apply proposal**.
- **Roleplay:** hints under Post-history instructions, Depth prompt and Role; under the soul's Class and start values; on **Story, move**. Lorebooks, Personas and Shortcuts get small **i** buttons that open on a click or a tap. "Test keys" is **Test triggers**.
- **Library** is one name now; Litopys is introduced once as the chronicler that writes the chapters, and an empty Library says what will appear and when.

### 🧹 One of each, and no false alarms (Roleplay 4.39.0)
The stock preset shows one quiet notice and one **Create editable copy** button, and its read-only rows show a lock instead of a pencil. The Soul tab has one rating button; **Delete soul** moved into the ⋯ menu. Switches are "on" in the theme's second colour instead of red, so red means destructive again. Home no longer shows the same three buttons twice.

### 🛡️ Safer settings
Deleting a connection asks first and says the key and its models go with it. Desktop Settings has a close button. Roleplay's **Appearance** settings are split into Text, Layout and Motion and have **Restore defaults**.

### 📱 Better on a phone
List rows that open a page have a chevron, buttons are at least 40 px, template titles wrap instead of being cut, Molfar's header names its icons, and the Daylight Home text is readable over the portrait. Roleplay's **no model** chip shows on phones too.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- The Groups tab's title says "Groups" with the group count, not "Characters 0".
- "1 chats" is "1 chat"; "tok" is "tokens"; card kinds read "Single" / "Narrator".
- A missing portrait shows a silhouette and "No portrait" instead of a "?" that looked broken.
- In a narrator-mode group chat, the narrator's replies show the group's portrait.
- A chat titled with the character's name no longer shows the name twice in the Chats list.

## Other changes
- Shell: connection rows say Connected / Needs key; the models counter reads "N shown · M available"; "Add connection" is the main button; the New app dialog has labels; the theme menu shows a swatch per theme; an app's delete button is a trash icon.
- Molfar's page: chats in the list show their time; Continue sits on its own row above the templates; the context shows once.
- Roleplay: the card editor shows "Saved" after an edit; the Chats list has a **New chat** button; the New chat dialog explains what a preset is.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.39.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. It works on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.3".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.3`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.3-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.3-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.3` or `:latest`.
