# 🏠 Molfar Vertep 1.16.4

A small engine fix that carries two app updates: **Roleplay 4.116.0** (Skip time, characters stop knowing what only your Persona card says) and **Social 0.15.1**. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then press Update on **Roleplay** and **Social** in Updates.

## ⏩ Roleplay 4.116.0: Skip time
The chat's "+" menu has a new **Skip time…**. Pick a number and a unit (minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years) and, if you like, a note ("until the morning", "the train arrives"). With no number, the note alone says how long. It sends one stage direction and nothing in your own words, so the next reply jumps over the gap and goes on from its end, and the Dashboard clock follows what the reply says.

## 🎭 Characters no longer know what only your Persona card says
Models kept letting strangers mention things that only the Persona card holds (what your character made, owns or did). The Persona text now reaches the model under one line (about 45 tokens, every preset) saying it is your character's sheet, known to the narrator, and that other characters know of it only what the story showed them; one who has not met your character knows none of it. The Persona memory (earlier stories) says the same about a character who was not there. This was not tried on a real model: free models may still ignore it, so tell us if you still see it.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Roleplay:** a regex script over a text longer than 20,000 characters no longer drops everything after them; the pattern still sees the first 20,000 characters and the rest is kept as it was.
- **Roleplay:** the Agents drawer is titled "Agents", not "Canvas".
- **Social:** a messenger chat whose reply failed (or is not due yet) keeps growing its memory note; before, it stopped until you retried.

## Other changes
- **Engine:** the embeddings a plugin receives are lined up with the texts it sent, with `null` for a blank text, so `texts[i]` pairs with `vectors[i]`. Roleplay's lorebook embed pass could be shifted by one entry when the scan window was blank.
- Roleplay: the lorebook budget rule ("stop at the first entry that does not fit") and the UI catalog status are written down in its docs (#397 asks whether short later entries should be allowed to fill the room).
- Social: the check-in after three quiet days reads its own constant; docs match the code.

## Known limits
- Skip time and the Persona line were not tried on a real model, and Skip time not in the light theme.
- The guide translations of Roleplay's `memory` page (13 languages) were written without a native read.

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page. Apps: Updates > Roleplay > Update, then Social. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
