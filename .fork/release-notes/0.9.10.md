# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.9.10

Two fixes reported on our Discord: Molfar now always runs on the model you picked, and replies from endpoints like Chub's Soji no longer vanish halfway.

## 🛠️ Fixes

### 🎯 Molfar runs on the model you picked, or tells you why not
When the model picked on Molfar's page was missing from the engine's model list at that moment, Molfar quietly took another one: the project's model, the default from Settings, or in the end the alphabetically first model. On OpenRouter that is often a Claude model, so one player was billed for Claude while Kimi K2.5 was selected.
Now Molfar never swaps a model. If the picked model (or the project's or the default one) is not available, you get a clear message naming it: "The model … is not available: its connection is missing, offline, or has no usable key. Pick another model." The engine also refreshes the live model lists before it picks.

### 💬 Replies from Chub's Soji and similar endpoints stay
Some OpenAI-compatible endpoints end their reply stream without the usual "finish" marker. Molfar Vertep treated that as a failed reply and threw away the text that had already appeared, with "model error: Stream ended without finish_reason". On custom OpenAI-compatible connections the end of the stream now counts as a finished reply, in Roleplay and in Molfar alike. Endpoints that do send the marker work as before.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.9.10".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.9.10`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.10-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.10-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.9.10` or `:latest`.
