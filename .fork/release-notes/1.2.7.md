# ✨ Molfar Vertep 1.2.7

A small release: the Polish button finally works, and Roleplay gets a light built-in preset. Both come with Roleplay 4.56.0, an app update.

### ✍️ Polish my message
Write a draft and press **Polish** next to Send (or **+ → Compose**). The chat's own model fixes spelling and grammar and makes the text more vivid: a precise verb, a sensory detail, a touch of feeling. It keeps every action and spoken line, your language, your person (I, he, she) and your tense, and it never acts for other characters. The result goes back into the box, nothing is sent, and **Undo** brings your draft back. On a phone the button is an icon.

The instruction it uses is a preset utility prompt, **Polish**, under Presets → Utility prompts. Leave it blank for the built-in one.

### 🪶 Vertep Lite, a light preset
Molfar and FRANKENX weigh about 13k tokens. **Vertep Lite** does the job in about 2k (under 3k with the heaviest options), which leaves room for the story on models with a small context.
- **Long replies by default**, with a length choice from short to epic in Choices.
- **A hidden micro plan**: before each reply the model writes a few lines in the story's language (where we are, what moves, what is new). The plan goes into the thinking block, not the story.
- **The Muse**: one fresh creative spark per reply, so scenes do not settle into the same rhythm. You can turn it off.
- **Your choices**: point of view, tense, style, difficulty, NSFW, story language and how much the model may do for your character.
- **Made for Vertep**: it knows the notes Litopys, the Dashboard and the World DM add to the prompt and gives them their place.
- **The prompt cache holds**: everything that changes per reply comes after your newest message, so the long part of the request stays the same.

Pick it for a chat in Presets; it appears after the Roleplay update.

### 🌍 Also in Roleplay since 1.2.6
Roleplay 4.55.0 came out as an app update after the last release. **New world** (World → Worlds) builds a world before any chat, from the cards you pick or a short interview, and any characters can then start a chat in it. The **Characters** page has one bar for Characters and Groups, search, sort, view, Import and New.
