# 🧭 Molfar Vertep 1.2.6

The World DM gets its own section: build a world before the story starts, keep it, take it into new chats, and give it backgrounds. This comes with Roleplay 4.54.0, an app update.

### 🌍 A World section of its own
The rail has a **World** item with three tabs. **Chats** lists every chat with a DM, **Worlds** holds the worlds you saved, and **Profiles** holds the DM's instructions. A chat's world opens on Overview, Records, Start, Backgrounds and Runs. Runs shows each DM call with its tokens and, when one fails, the reason in plain words.

### 🎭 DM profiles instead of fixed levels
The levels (Chronicler, Living world, Director, Antagonist, Total chaos) became profiles you can copy and edit: four prompt blocks, while the reply format stays locked so the DM keeps working. Options per profile: clocks ticked by code instead of by the model, news worded by how far a clock has run, and an events deck (off by default).

### 📜 Records you can see, edit and pin
The Records tab lists what the DM wrote down, and you can add, edit, retire or delete records yourself. What you change is marked "by you".

Each record now comes into the story by real keys. The DM writes 2 to 5 for each: a short name, what the characters call it, its main word. Before, a record's only key was its full name, so it rarely came up. **Add keys to N records** gives older records their keys in one call. **Always in the prompt** pins a record that should never be missing, such as the house the story lives in.

### 🏗️ Start a world before the first message
On the **Start** tab the DM's build model reads the chat's cards (description, personality, scenario and their enabled lorebook entries, in pieces a small model can read) or asks you a few questions. It writes a draft of the world: you remove what you do not want, then accept. It can also write the chat's own scenario and first message. The scenario replaces the card's, or is added to it.

### 💾 Saved worlds
**Save world** keeps a chat's world in the library. **New chat in this world** (or the World step of a new chat) starts another chat in it with any characters you like: records and fronts as they stand, clocks included.

### 🖼️ Backgrounds for a world
A world can have its own folder of backgrounds. **Make a set** plans the pictures with the build model and draws them with your image model (one per place by default, up to three).
- Places come from the world's records, from the card's lorebooks, or from **Find places in the cards**.
- A vision model can name and tag the pictures you already have.
- A chat can **change the background by scene**: the Dashboard picks the picture that fits the place and mood. This is off by default.

New backgrounds are stored as WebP in the engine's asset store instead of inside the app's library file. **Extensions → Backgrounds** adds a folder filter, tags, and **Convert to WebP** for the old ones. GIFs and animated pictures stay as they are, and so does a picture that WebP would not make smaller.

### 📬 Failures you can see
When a background job fails (the Dashboard, the Library, the World DM or a soul rating), the Inbox counts it and a notice says so, in plain words for the common causes.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **The Dashboard after an earlier change** (#48). Editing, continuing, deleting or swiping an earlier message left later stats, threads, clock and scene computed from text that was gone. The Dashboard now reads the story again from that message, in one call, and the line under the newest message says from which one. Swiping back to a swipe it already read costs nothing. (From Molfar's workspace, reworked so an old branch never forces a whole-chat re-read.)
- **Streaming with a regex that hides thinking.** The reply jumped between the story and the reasoning on every tick. Now the text only grows, and a thinking block stays hidden while it is written.
- **Card editor.** The Translate button pressed itself on a click anywhere on its label row, and a tab sometimes kept the panel that was open.

## Other changes
- **Card editor** (#63, from Molfar's workspace): the eleven tabs sit in five groups on a phone (Core, Profile, Soul, Lorebook, More); on a wide screen every panel stays one click away, and the tab row is one keyboard stop.
- **The prompt Inspector** keeps the last 50 requests instead of 20, so the reply is still there after the Dashboard, World DM and Litopys calls that follow it.
