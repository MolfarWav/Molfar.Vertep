# 🧬 Molfar Vertep 1.9.4

Characters now **know less than the player**: a character no longer uses a secret only you, or another character, knows. This is **Roleplay 4.83.0, an app update**; the engine itself has no code changes in this release. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then open Updates and press Update on Roleplay.

## 🤫 A character's reply carries only what that character knows
- Before, the story record (Litopys) handed every character's reply the facts that *anyone present, or you* knew, so a fact only you knew, or only another character knew, could reach a character who had no way to know it. Now a character's reply gets the facts that character knows, or that everyone knows. It applies in one-to-one chats, in groups where each member speaks, and in the backstory of a chat that continues an earlier one.
- A reply that voices several characters (a narrator card, a group in narrator mode) still gets what anyone present knows, because one voice has to tell the whole scene.
- When the model that writes the chapters says nothing usable about who knows a new fact, the fact is now known to the people of that scene, not to everyone.

## 🗒️ "Who knows what" list for a narrator
- A reply that voices several characters now carries a short list (250 tokens at most, no extra model call) of the facts only some of those present know, grouped as "Only Aria and Bram know: …". It is **on by default** and can be switched off in Settings > Memory (the Recollections block, "Who knows what, for a narrator").
- We measured it on 16 synthetic scenes with three models, 24 replies each. A secret leaked in 14 narrator replies on a local 9B model without the list and in 4 with it; on DeepSeek V4 Flash 3 became 0; Gemma 4 31B did not leak either way. Small samples, one judge model, so read it as a direction.
- In a character's own reply the filter above does the job; the same 9B leaked a secret in 16 of 24 replies when it was in the prompt and in 0 when the filter kept it out.

## 📓 Other things that follow
- A group in narrator mode now gets each present character's own notebook (what they learned about your character), named, as a narrator card already did.
- A Data Bank file attached to one character goes into a group reply only when that character speaks, or when the reply voices the whole group. Before, any member's file went in whenever the member was in the group.

## Not done yet
- Chapters you did not witness are still shown in full to every voice; a speaker-by-speaker filter for them, and a check on replies that leak anyway, are next.
- The settings switch was not looked at on a phone yet.
