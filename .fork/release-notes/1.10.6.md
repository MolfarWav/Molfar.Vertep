# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 1.10.6

Voices get their own colors, and the Dashboard stops stalling on long chats. Both are **Roleplay 4.90.0, an app update**; the engine itself is unchanged. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then open Updates and press Update on Roleplay.

## 🎨 Colors for every voice
- In a chat with several characters, each one's lines now get their own color, all different, kept with the chat. A card that holds several characters does it by itself, with no list to type. The characters are the ones the Dashboard already knows (one person stays one person, whether the story writes the name in Latin or Cyrillic, in full or short).
- In any other chat: the chat menu has **Voice colors**. Turn it on, pick a color for a character, send one back to automatic, or press **Spread colors** to give everyone you have not picked a different color. A new character gets the next free color, so no two share one.
- Colors are handed out only where nobody chose. A card with its own list of Voices, or its own Dialogue highlight, is left alone (turn the switch on by hand to color it anyway). In a group, a member's chosen color is respected, the others fill the gaps, and more than eight members all differ.
- This needs the Relationship dashboard, which supplies the characters.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **The Dashboard could stop updating on a long chat** (#245): "The scene state could not be updated … exceeded the maximum allowed time limit", on every reply, on a phone and on a desktop. Roleplay 4.88.0 gave every thing and place of an old chat an id inside one 10-second step, and since nothing is saved until the step ends, each reply started over. The same comparisons were made tens of thousands of times; they are now made once, and the second step of an update no longer reads the replies again. Measured in the app's own sandbox: the first update of a 40-snapshot chat 2.5 s (it was over 10 s), a real chat of six characters about 0.9 s per update (3.4 to 5.4 s before). A phone is several times slower than the machine this was measured on, so a very long old chat may still be tight there.

## Other changes
- A heading with a role in front of the name on an imported card ("The Class Representative Yuki Tanaka") is shown and matched by the name after it.
- The guide page "Characters, personas and groups" and its translations cover the new colors.

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart, then Update on Roleplay in Updates. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
