# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.9.7

See what your prompt is made of. Every part of a request now says where it came from: the card, the preset section, the lorebook entry and why it fired, each history message, what Litopys and the dashboard added, and what was left out. Sends also stay fast with big lorebooks and heavy presets, which fixes "script execution has exceeded the maximum allowed time limit" on phones. With Roleplay 4.32.0.

## ✨ What's new

### 🔍 Where each part of the prompt comes from
Open Molfar's page, the prompt inspector (the magnifier button), and pick a Roleplay reply. Above the text there is now a **Sources** table:
- the parts grouped by kind (Card, Persona, Preset, Lorebook, History, Memory, Dashboard, Tools…), each with its tokens and share of the request;
- every preset section by name, every lorebook entry with why it fired (`key: tower`, constant, by meaning…), every history message, with the regex scripts that changed it;
- a click on a row marks that part in the text and scrolls to it; Esc clears the mark.

Below the table:
- **Left out**: what did not make it and why: sections switched off, gated by a condition or not used for this kind of reply, lorebook entries that did not fit or were filtered out, how many entries' keys were not in the text, history the context budget trimmed, messages Litopys keeps as chapters.
- **Preset choices**: the options the chat is using.

Handy when a reply ignores the lore you expected, or to see what eats your context window on a small model.

### 🪟 The same in Prompt peek (Roleplay 4.32.0)
Roleplay's **Prompt peek** (a message's ⋯ menu, or Presets) shows the same table and marks for the request the next reply would send. Plugins (Litopys, the dashboard) add their parts only when the message is really sent, so those appear in Molfar's inspector.

### 🧙 Molfar's own requests, labeled
Molfar's requests show their parts too: the rules, the workspace layout, the installed apps, each AGENTS.md and data guide, your notes, memory, the project and your personal instructions, the conversation by role, and how many tokens each tool definition takes. Useful to see what a long session costs before you send the next message.

### ⚡ Fast sends with big lorebooks (Roleplay 4.32.0)
On the Android app, a chat with a big lorebook (a simulator card's built-in book, for example) could fail on every send with "script execution has exceeded the maximum allowed time limit", whatever the preset or model. Each lorebook key was checked with a slow pattern on every send. A 300-entry book took about 3 s on a desktop (several times that on a phone), and a 1000-entry book ran past the limit even on a desktop. Now they take about 0.3 s and 0.6 s, with the same entries firing as before.

Presets that bring many regex scripts and long chats got faster too: a 400-message chat with 150 regex scripts went from 3.3 s to 1.3 s per send on a desktop.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- The prompt inspector counted the opening system messages twice, so their share of the request looked bigger than it was.
- The inspector keeps much longer texts (200k characters of a system prompt, 50k of a message; was 20k), so parts near the end of a long prompt can be shown.
- When an app's request fails, the error now names which request it was (for example `route POST /chats/…/send failed: …`), not just "route failed".
- Roleplay: counts take the right word form ("1 entry", "1 global book"; in Ukrainian "1 повідомлення", "5 повідомлень").

## Other changes
- Apps can label the parts of their model requests for the inspector (`promptSources`, never sent to the model); the `app-authoring` skill tells Molfar how.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.9.7". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.32.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.9.7`.
- **Downloads** below: `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.7-<system>` for desktops and `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.7-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer (installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data).
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.9.7` or `:latest`.
