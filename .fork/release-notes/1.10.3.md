# 📖 Molfar Vertep 1.10.3

Ten more Help articles, help inside the agent editor, a message-format block for Vertep Lite, working dialogue colours for a card, a length for character recollections, and a fix for a reset button that deleted more than it said. This is **Roleplay 4.87.0, an app update**; the engine itself has no code changes in this release. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then open Updates and press Update on Roleplay.

## 📚 Ten more Help articles
- New articles, in English and Ukrainian, for screens a newcomer meets early: **starting a new chat**, the **Chats page** (search, sort, filter, select, move, delete), **Manage chat files** (export as text or JSONL, make a checkpoint, import), **What this chat carries** (what goes into the prompt besides the history, and the switch for each part), the **"+" menu** by the message box, **Sprites** (pictures that follow a character's mood), **Backup and restore**, the **Appearance and Chat Behavior switches**, **Arcs** in the Library (when old chapters merge into one, and how to undo it) and **Customizing the Home page**.
- Each article says what the screen offers, what to press, what is on by default and what it does not do. The Help now has 79 articles; every claim in them points at the line of code or text that proves it, and a script checks that.

## 🧩 The agent editor explains its cards
- Select a card and its **Card tab** has a folded block, **"What goes in and out"**: what the card takes in and what it gives out, in plain words, with "optional" where a connection may stay empty. It follows the card's own settings, so an Input card lists what it gives and a template lists its slots.
- Three new Help articles: **how an agent works** (a card, a wire, a run, what the check and "N model calls per run" mean), **the cards** (every type, which ones cost a model call) and **three ready agents** (Tension meter, Next moves, Pacing nudge: which cards, where the result shows, what it costs).
- The help lives in the card's panel and not as a "?" on the card: a card is a small, draggable target, and on a phone a "?" there is too small to tap.

## 🎭 Message format in Vertep Lite
- A new pick in Vertep Lite's Choices, **Message format**, **on by default**: the classic roleplay look in every reply. Speech in quotation marks, actions and narration of what the scene shows in *asterisks*, a character's thoughts in asterisks too and said to be thoughts, each speaker's lines in a new paragraph. **Off** sends no format rule, so the card, its examples and the chat history set the look.
- The formatting sentence that was part of the Form block moved into this one (the rule against headings, lists and notes stays in Form). A copy of the preset that is still read-only takes the change on update: before, narration was plain text and only thoughts were italic. About 120 tokens more in the request. A copy you made editable keeps its text and does not get the new pick.

## 🎨 A card's dialogue colour now shows
- A card's **Dialogue highlight** (Characters > the card > Colors) did nothing in its own chats: it was used only for a group member's replies and for the labelled lines of your own message, so an ordinary card's quotes always took the theme's colour. It is now the base colour of the quotes in that card's replies. A voice that is named on a line still paints that line in the voice's colour. A card where the colour was set but never showed will now show it.

## 💭 How long a recollection is
- A character's recollection of a scene ("What they remember") was cut at exactly 300 characters, often in the middle of a word, and no setting changed that: "Recollections budget" is the room all recollections together may take in a reply, not the length of one. There is now **Length of one recollection** under it (Settings > Memory, 150 to 1000 characters, default 300 as before). The chapter writer is told the length, a longer text is cut after its last whole sentence (or at a word), and the editor follows the setting. It applies to scenes written from now on and to your edits; recollections already written keep their text.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **"Reset look and local settings" no longer deletes your folders, your Backgrounds list and your connection profiles.** Its dialog promised to reset only the appearance settings, themes, quick replies and tags, but the reset emptied those collections too and saved that to the engine, so they were gone for good. It now does only what the dialog says. Chats that had already lost their folders do not get them back; restore a backup if you have one (Settings > Backup).
- The guide page about agents said an agent can run before, with or after a reply, or by hand. The editor runs agents **after every reply** only; the page now says so, and its 13 translations follow.

## Other changes
