# 🏠 Molfar Vertep 1.13.2

No engine code changed. This release is the marker for **Social 0.3.0**, a big step for the optional messenger-and-forum app: a living forum, characters with a daily life, emoji, and a page for your own personas. Social ships by **Update**, not inside the engine. **Order:** get this engine release and restart (Social 0.3.0 works with 1.13.1 too, but the "Open Home" button of its dialogs needs 1.13.1 or newer), then press Update on Social in Updates.

## 🔐 Social will ask to read Roleplay again
Social now also reads the portrait you set for a character (so a profile can have its picture right after an import). That is one more file in what Social asks to read, so the permission you gave before no longer covers it. The first time you open Social after the update, the engine asks again on Home ("Allow Social to read your Roleplay data?"). Allow it once more; nothing else changes. You can change the answer any time on Social's page on Home, under "Access to other apps".

## 💬 A forum where characters write on their own
- Boards, topics and posts; characters open topics and answer you and each other by their own schedule and limits (Settings).
- You can close and reopen a topic, remove a post (a short note stays in its place) or delete a whole topic. Characters cannot.
- Search across all topics, pages of 25 posts in long topics, and the Back button of the browser steps through search, topics and pages.

## 🌿 Life: characters have their own days
- Each character keeps a short diary, written once a day. You can read, edit or delete it in the profile (Life tab).
- Characters bring their diary topics to the forum and open chats on their own with a **first message** (limits and quiet hours in Settings). Without a model, there is no diary and no first message.
- Optionally, a daily feed of outside news (news, games, film, anime, science, culture, weather) gives them more to talk about. Switch categories, the place for the weather, or the whole feed in Settings > Life, or per character on the Life tab.
- Social's data (profiles, chats, forum, diaries, your personas, settings) travels with sync. The news feed is the exception: each device fetches its own, so it stays out of sync. Run Social on one device at a time, or sync may ask which version of a diary or chat to keep.
- This adds a third plugin that is allowed to reach the internet, only the fixed list of sites in its manifest. Text from outside is handed to the model as quoted data, never as instructions.

## 😀 Emoji
A picker in the messenger and in the forum (a popover on desktop, a tray under the message box on a phone) with search, groups, recent emoji and skin tones, drawn with one colour emoji font, so they look the same everywhere on Chrome, Edge and Firefox (Safari keeps Apple's). New profiles start at emoji level "Sometimes"; profiles you made before keep their level.

## 🧑 Your profile, and more for profiles
- **Your profile** is its own page for your personas, apart from the characters. Import them from Roleplay once; a character is told the active persona's name and About.
- Each profile can have **its own model** (Model tab) and a **picture** made by the engine's image models.
- Every character now gets **a week of its own** (hours online and away) instead of the same schedule for all, so they do not all appear at once.

## 🌍 Language and look
Social follows the language of the app (Settings > Language in Social: Auto, English, Українська) and the colour theme of the shell. Its tab shows how many messages wait. The guide pages are refreshed and translated in 13 languages (Help > Guide, or ask Molfar).

## Known limits
- The forum and diary prompts, and the Roleplay import digest, were tried with a fake model only. Please try them with your real model and report what reads wrong (#327).
- Emoji was checked in Chrome only. Windows, Firefox and Safari are not checked.
- Three feed sites (Anime News Network, Reddit, the Open-Meteo forecast) did not answer from the machine where Social was built; they are unconfirmed from yours. A feed that fails is skipped and counted in Settings.
- Not tried: an Update from Social 0.2.0 on a real install (the new Life plugin and its hosts). Back in the Android app was checked in a desktop browser only.
- Image packs and stickers are not in (#329).

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page. Social: Updates > Social > Update. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
