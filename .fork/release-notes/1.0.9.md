# 🧭 Molfar Vertep 1.0.9

Molfar can now fix a bug you report, moving your profile to another device keeps your apps official, and unsent drafts are much harder to lose. Roleplay 4.46.0 adds a short guide to what is new and a batch of fixes from a day of testing on a phone. Many of the fixes come from a bug tracker a player shared on Discord: thank you.

### 🐞 Ask Molfar first
The **Report a bug** dialog has a new block: **Ask Molfar first**. Describe what happened and Molfar opens a new chat with your report, the version and recent errors. It saves a checkpoint, tries to reproduce the bug and makes the smallest fix, and you try it. If it works, its card offers **It works, send the fix**: a pre-filled solution form on GitHub with the description and the code change as a patch. If it could not fix the bug, you get the ordinary report with what it found. Nothing leaves your computer by itself: you read everything on GitHub before you post it.

### 📦 Moving to another device just works
After you restore a profile from a backup, for example to move from a desktop to a phone, an official app such as Roleplay used to count as unofficial. Its links to Settings and to Molfar were refused with "not allowed for this app", and only a future update could fix that. Now the engine checks a restored app against its official repository by itself. If the code matches, the app is official again and you never notice. If you changed the app's code, its page says **Check needed** and offers **Replace with official version**, with a confirm first. Your app data is never touched. If the repository did not answer, you get **Check again**.

### 💾 Drafts that stay put
Your unsent text is saved to the shell, and now the shell confirms each save. A reloaded app no longer comes back with an older draft than the shell holds. A draft the browser refuses to store, because the app's share or the browser's storage is full, is no longer reported as saved: in Roleplay 4.46.0 an amber line says it is not saved and gives you **Copy**. Roleplay also stopped spending saves on every streamed word, which used to leave a reload with an old copy.

### 🧭 What's new and short tours (Roleplay 4.46.0)
After an update, Roleplay shows what changed since your last version, one tip at a time. Short tours point out the Canvas links, the Prompt board and the composer the first time you open them. Turn tips off or show them again in Settings → Guide. English and Ukrainian.

### 📱 A two-row composer on phones (Roleplay 4.46.0)
On a phone the composer takes two rows, not three: your text, then "+", **Write for me**, the big editor and Send. Continue and Another are in the "+" menu. On a desktop all the quick moves stay.

### ✍️ Write for me never writes over you (Roleplay 4.46.0)
A Write for me result that arrives late goes only into the chat you asked in, and only if your draft has not changed since. Otherwise it is offered to you, not pasted over what you typed. A message that could not be sent comes back into the composer with its attachments.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **No more reloads for nothing.** An edit that no part of an app's interface uses, such as a test or a doc, left the app marked "needs a build", so the next open rebuilt it and reloaded the page. An unchanged build now records itself.
- **Roleplay 4.46.0: Dashboard and Litopys on long chats.** Notes and facts are matched against the scene indexed once, so a long chat with many entries no longer runs past the script time limit and leaves the tracker stale.
- **Roleplay 4.46.0: Profile, Structure it** no longer fails on a long card with "not JSON" (the reply had been cut off). If it still fails, the message says whether the reply was cut off or in the wrong format.
- **Roleplay 4.46.0: the context ring** in the chat header counts against the model's context window, as the request does. It used the preset's 8192 and could show over 100 %.
- **Roleplay 4.46.0: number fields** no longer turn an emptied field into 0 with your digits typed after it ("07500").
- **Roleplay 4.46.0:** an old "trimmed" Dashboard notice goes away once a reply fits again.
- **The app page header fits a phone:** the name and Launch share the first row and the other actions wrap below; nothing is cut off at 390 px.

## Other changes
- App frames: `window.chrysalisStorage.unsaved()` lists keys the shell refused, with a `chrysalis:storage` event; each storage write is acknowledged by the shell.
- `POST /v1/apps/:id/build/ack` (shell only): an unchanged build records its revision for the lease holder.
- The update checks report `restored` for official apps restored from a backup, and clear it when the code matches the repository.
- New agent tool `bug_solution` and built-in skill `fix-bug`; `GET /v1/agent/bug-patch` gives the app's code since a checkpoint as a patch, never data or memory.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.46.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. It works on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0 and later; the "not saved" warning needs 1.0.9.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.9".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.9`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.9-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.9-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.9` or `:latest`.
