# 💞 Molfar Vertep 0.7.0

Roleplay learns to keep track of relationships: who trusts you, who knows what about you, and what that does to the next reply. Your apps, chats and settings are kept.

## ✨ What's new

### 💞 Roleplay 4.22.0: the relationship dashboard (through app updates)
Open **Updates** in the top bar to install Roleplay 4.22.0. After your replies a small model call reads the turn and names what happened: a kept promise, a compliment, a threat. Code, not the model, turns that into each character's attitude to you (trust, comfort, attraction, respect, affection), their excitement, the scene's clock and place, the open story threads, and a notebook of what each character knows about you and how they learned it: saw it, heard it from someone, or only guesses. Before the next reply the story model gets a few plain words about how the speaking character feels and what they know (never numbers), so a character who has not heard your name will not use it, and one who distrusts you acts like it. Swipes and rewinds are followed: a discarded swipe leaves nothing behind. The reading runs in the background about a minute after a reply; **Refresh** runs it at once. It uses the chat's model unless you pick another in Tools → Relationship dashboard, where you also set how many tokens the words may take.

### 🌟 A strip beside the chat, a full view, and a sheet on phones
On a computer, a strip beside the chat shows the time and place, who is present, the character's **constellation** (the shape of your relationship, such as Friendship, Dangerous pull or Honor without trust) and five attitude bars. **Open dashboard** shows everything: what each bar means in words, what changed this turn and the arithmetic behind it, your story's turning points with a chart of the last ten turns, the character's notebook and blind spot, the others in the scene and how they relate to each other. On phones a one-line bar under the chat header opens the same as tabs. The strip collapses to a thin rail when you want the room.

### 🪶 A Soul for every main character
Character cards get a **Soul** tab: whether the character starts as a love interest, an ally, neutral or hostile, how they feel about you at the start, and the traits that make them warm up or take offence faster, plus what hurts them most and what they prize. Roleplay rates a card's main characters by itself after import and shows it as a proposal you accept, change or dismiss; **Rate now** or **Rate with Molfar** do it on demand. Minor figures the story brings in never get one.

### 🧭 More room and order in Roleplay
Made with Molfar by a user and shipped to everyone: the left rail can hide its labels (a caret at its top), the persona filter on chat lists offers every persona, presets get a **Regex** tab with the scripts that run only under them, the Regex list is grouped by preset, character and chat, and the Tools tabs are grouped.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **NanoGPT models that cannot switch thinking off work in plugins again.** NanoGPT refuses a request that turns thinking off for some models ("Invalid value for reasoning.effort ... none"); the engine now retries at the lowest level the error lists, so the dashboard and Litopys work with them.
- **An app's draft for Molfar is no longer lost** when the agent page already had a chat open ("Rate with Molfar" and other "Ask Molfar" buttons).

## Other changes
- For app makers: a plugin route's model request can carry `turn` labels (who speaks, and whether it is a new reply, a swipe, a continue or an impersonation). The model never sees them; `llmRequest` hooks get them as `ctx.turn`.
- The launcher's `dev` mode works when a git worktree holds the branch, and `dev NAME` follows the branch `claude/NAME`.
- The README has the Molfar Vertep logo and a short tour of what is inside.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.7.0". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.22.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.7.0`.
- **Downloads** below: `Chrysalis-0.7.0-<system>` (file names stay as before so self-update keeps working). The Android APK is debug-signed and cannot update an installed copy.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.7.0` or `:latest`.
