# 🧭 Molfar Vertep 1.0.8

See and shape what goes into every request, build the chat map one chat at a time, and write faster under a long draft. Most of it comes with the Roleplay app 4.44.0 and 4.45.0. Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 now show up for Anthropic connections.

### 🧩 The Prompt board (Roleplay 4.44.0)
Canvas has a second board: **Prompt**. Pick a chat and you see every part of its request in order: main prompt, preset sections, card fields, persona, each lorebook, chat history, data bank, author's note, Litopys and the Dashboard, each with its tokens, wired into "Request to the model" with the total. Switch any part **off for this chat only**: your preset and card stay as they are. Parts the preset, a condition or the reply type leave out say so on their card, and the details tell you what was taken and why something was left out, with the text itself. Changes save at once and Ctrl+Z undoes them. On a phone, the List view has the switches.

### 🕰️ A history filter (Roleplay 4.44.0)
On the Chat history card: send only the last N messages, only the player's or only the character's, and drop thinking blocks if you like. It applies before older messages are cut to fit the context.

### 💸 Switched off means no work (Roleplay 4.44.0)
A Litopys or Dashboard you switch off for a chat does nothing for it at all, and a lorebook you switch off is not scanned, even a global one or one bound to your persona. Small context windows get that room back.

### 🔗 The chat map, one chat at a time (Roleplay 4.44.0)
The Chat links board starts empty. A list on the left holds the chats that are not on it, with search and sort. Drag a chat onto the board, use "+", or put it there with all its linked chats; drag a card back onto the list to take it off. The view stays where it is after a drop or a move.

### ⚡ Quick moves under a long draft (Roleplay 4.45.0)
Once your draft wraps, three coloured buttons sit under it: **Continue**, **Write for me** and **Another**, plus a button for the big editor. They were only in the "+" menu. Write for me replaces the draft with a written message, and Undo in the notice brings your draft back. The big editor can now take half the screen, so the chat stays in view, or all of it.

### 📱 Tidier phone pages (Roleplay 4.45.0)
**Characters:** the Characters / Groups switch and New on top, then search, Filter and More. Filter opens a sheet with the sort and every tag, wrapped, instead of a long row you swipe sideways. **Lorebooks:** a filter next to the search (all, global, on a card, not linked), and Import and "Active in the open chat" in a More menu instead of the bottom bar. Group editor tabs show their names.

### 🤖 Claude 5.5 for Anthropic connections
Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 now appear in the model list of Anthropic connections, including a subscription sign-in. They were missing because the built-in Anthropic catalog stopped at Opus 5 and Sonnet 5.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Roleplay 4.45.0: pale dialogue colours on light themes.** Group voices are darkened on light themes so the yellow and the other light colours read well; dark themes are unchanged.
- **Roleplay 4.45.0: a confusing empty Library.** A chat whose record has nothing yet shows one explanation of when the first chapter comes, with Open the chat, instead of "Up to date" over two "none yet" lines.
- **Names:** the browser tab, a few texts, the profile download and examples in Settings say Molfar Vertep instead of Chrysalis.

## Other changes
- Roleplay 4.45.0: five more Dashboard events seen on real chats (introducing oneself, inviting to meet, standing one's ground, an ultimatum, a veiled hold over someone).
- Roleplay 4.45.0: Soul rates on the spot with Rate now / Rate again; the "Rate with Molfar" buttons are gone. Advanced fields in the character editor say what they do.
- Roleplay 4.44.0: a chat's prompt switches live in its meta (`promptBoard`); the prompt preview lists what a chat can switch and keys every part. See the app's `docs/DATA-FORMATS.md`.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.44.0 and 4.45.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. They work on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0 and later.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.8".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.8`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.8-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.8-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.8` or `:latest`.

Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) and the [Roleplay CHANGELOG](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep-Roleplay/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
