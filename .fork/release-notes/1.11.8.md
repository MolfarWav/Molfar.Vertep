# 🧠 Molfar Vertep 1.11.8

No engine code changed. **Roleplay 4.99.0 arrives as an app update**: a finished story can go to the Archive, and a chat that another chat continues goes there by itself.

## 📦 Archive a finished story
- In Chats, press the amber **Archive** button in a chat's panel (or tick several with Select; inside a chat it is in the menu under "Manage chat"). The chat leaves the list and Home. An amber **Archive** row under the filters opens them; **Unarchive** brings one back.
- It is a status, not a move: the chat keeps its place, so its memory, its links on the Canvas and any book made from it keep working. An archived chat opens read only: a banner, no message box (a button "Continue in a new chat" stands in its place), no editing, swiping, deleting or regenerating, its preset fixed. You can read, copy, branch from a message (a branch is a normal chat), export, and look at its Dashboard and Library. The engine refuses writes to it, so an old tab cannot change it either.
- The confirmation offers **Archive and continue** when no chat continues it yet.
- An archived chat costs nothing in the background: opening it or pressing Refresh no longer runs the Dashboard sensor or the automatic soul rating.

## 🔗 The earlier chat goes to the Archive by itself
- When a chat becomes the earlier chat of another one (a link drawn on the Canvas, "Continue in a new chat" including from the popup that warns about a big request, or a pick in the new-chat step's "Continues"), it is archived at once and a toast with Undo says so. On the Canvas it is one toast, and its Undo brings back the link and the chat. Branches never count.
- Switch it off in Library > Litopys settings > "In the prompt" > "Archive the earlier chat when it is continued". On by default.
- A chat of 100 messages or more that has been quiet for a week gets a "Long chat · archive?" tag in the list and a hint in its panel. Nothing is archived for you.

## 📚 Docs
- The guide page on long chats has an Archive section. The translations of the whole Roleplay guide, in 13 languages, were refreshed (they had gone stale; names of screens stay in English, except in Ukrainian).

## Known and left
- The thresholds of the "Long chat" tag (100 messages, a week) are a first guess.
- The translations were made by a small model and read by structure, not by a native speaker; the pages "soul" and "canvas" of the book were not touched and may use older terms.
- On a busy engine the automatic archive may land a second or two after the new chat opens.

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart, then Update on Roleplay in Updates. `dev` only updates the engine, not the Roleplay app. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page.
