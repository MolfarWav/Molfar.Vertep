# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.9.3

The Marketplace opens up. Roleplay 4.28.0 adds five more card storefronts next to Chub, installs cards of any size with their emotion images, and translates cards for you. This engine version is what lets the big cards through.

## ✨ What's new

### 🏪 Six storefronts in the Marketplace (Roleplay 4.28.0)
Pick the source at the top of the Marketplace. Each one keeps its own search, page and filters:
- **Chub**: as before.
- **RisuRealm**: recommended, trending, newest, random or most downloaded; an adult switch.
- **CharaVault**: an archive of cards collected from other sites. Tags to include and exclude, creator, token range, "has a lorebook", where the card came from, ten orderings.
- **Wyvern** and **Pygmalion**: their public catalogs (SFW cards only without an account on those sites).
- **JannyAI**: search with tags and token range. JannyAI lets only your own browser download, so **Get on JannyAI** opens the card's page; download the PNG there and drop it on the bar above the grid.

Every card shows where it comes from, a gold **Lorebook** and a rose **Emotions** badge where the site says so.

### 🔗 Paste a card link
A box in the Marketplace and on the Characters page takes a link from RisuRealm, CharaVault, Wyvern, Pygmalion, GitHub, Hugging Face, Catbox or Discord and installs the card. Any other site: download the file and drop it in.

### 📦 Big cards and their emotion images
A RisuRealm card with 120 emotion images is often 50-150 MB. It now installs (in about a minute for the biggest), with the images as the character's expression sprites. charx files, PNG cards with images inside and JSON cards all work, and **Open character** takes you straight to it. The images are stored inside the card for now, so very large packs are made smaller to fit; full-size images as separate files come in a later version.

### 🌐 Translate a card
In a card's window in the Marketplace, **Translate** shows it in your language (English by default) and **Install translated** installs it that way, its lorebook too: the translated keys are added next to the original ones, so entries fire in both languages. A card you already have gets **Translate** in the character editor, and **Restore original** brings its own texts back. It uses the provider set in Roleplay's Tools → Translation.

### 😊 Emotion sprites
- Sprites now follow replies in **Ukrainian and Russian**, not only English.
- The floating sprite over the chat **folds** into a small chip (a click brings it back) and **resizes** from its corner; it remembers its size and place.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- PNG cards dropped on the Characters page or Home keep Cyrillic and Korean names intact (they came in garbled).
- V3 PNG cards that put their "ccv3" data first are read (they counted as "no card").
- A chub link imports from the card PNG itself, with all its fields.
- RisuRealm thumbnails show (their image server sends no image type).

## Other changes
- Apps can download files up to 200 MB through the engine (`GET /v1/apps/:appId/file`), limited to the hosts the app's plugins declare.
- The app bridge passes downloads as bytes instead of base64 text, so large files use a third of the memory.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.9.3". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.28.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.9.3`.
- **Downloads** below: `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.3-<system>` for desktops and `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.3-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer (installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data).
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.9.3` or `:latest`.
