# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 1.0.4

Group chats play the way you want, chat by chat. Everything here comes with the Roleplay app 4.40.0.

### 🎬 Each chat plays the group its own way (Roleplay 4.40.0)
How a group answers used to belong to the group card, so every chat of that group played the same. Now the group card holds the defaults, and each chat can change them for itself. Make a trio, play it with a narrator in one chat and let everyone answer in turn in another, without copying the group.

Above a group chat there is a **Turns** button. It names the current mode (**Narrator**, **Each in turn** or **You pick**) and opens the chat's settings: generation mode, who answers, self responses and auto mode. Muting a member and moving them left or right from their chip also change only this chat. A dot on the button means the chat has settings of its own; **Use the group's settings** takes it back. Anything a chat did not change keeps following the group, so editing the group still reaches those chats. A new group chat can pick its generation mode right in the new-chat dialog.

### 👆 Pick who speaks in one tap (Roleplay 4.40.0)
"Only when you trigger a member" is now **Only the member you pick**. In that mode a **Who answers?** row sits above the message box: tap a name and that member replies. Before, the only way was a member's chip, then "Trigger reply", so it looked like nobody ever answered.

### 💬 Settings that say what they do (Roleplay 4.40.0)
"Allow self responses" is **The same member may answer twice in a row**, "Auto mode" is **Keep the scene going on its own**, and each has a line underneath explaining it, in the chat's Turns and in the group editor. Self responses show only where they matter (who answers: Natural).

## 🛠️ Fixes
- A group switched to Narrator after "only the member you pick" never answered, because the hidden "who answers" stayed on manual. A narrator now always answers.
- After "you pick" was set, sending a message showed a reply bubble that vanished. Now only your message appears.
- Auto mode under "you pick" kept sending empty turns that nobody answered; it is hidden and paused there.
- Right after a reply, a group chat could briefly lose its turn settings (auto mode read as off).

## Other changes
- Roleplay: a group chat's own settings are stored in the chat as `turns` (see the app's `docs/DATA-FORMATS.md`); the group file is untouched.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.40.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. It works on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.4".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.4`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.4-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.4-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.4` or `:latest`.
