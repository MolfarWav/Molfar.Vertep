# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 1.0.2

Groups write their own scene, and the Chats page finally shows what each chat is about. Most of this comes with the Roleplay app 4.37.0 and 4.38.0; the engine adds one built-in skill for Molfar.

### 🎬 A group writes its own scene
The group editor's **Scene** tab can now write the shared scenario for you. Pick how: **From the cards** uses the members' own world, places and relationships; **Something wild** puts them somewhere unexpected while everyone stays in character. Add a wish if you like ("a rainy night at a mountain inn"); the scenario comes out in the language you write the wish in. Then **Generate opening** writes openings from that scenario, one at a time, each different from the ones the group already has. Every result arrives as a draft you can edit, use, retry or discard, and nothing changes until you accept it. The model of the group's latest chat writes it, so the scene sounds like the chats.

### 💬 A new Chats page
The chat list sits on the left, grouped into **Pinned**, **Today**, **This week** and **Earlier**, each heading in its own colour. Filter one-on-one or group chats, sort by recent, size or name, and use **Select** to move or delete several chats at once. The right side shows the chat you picked: Continue, Pin, New chat, the story so far from the Library (Litopys chapters), the last three messages in each speaker's colour, and the chat's persona, preset, model, history size, lorebooks and turns. On a phone the panel opens after a tap. Group chats keep their violet colour everywhere.

### 📌 Pinned chats
Pin a chat from its panel and it stays at the top of the Chats page. Pinning does not count as activity, so the chat keeps its place in time.

### 🎨 Groups stand apart (Roleplay 4.37.0)
Characters and Groups are two separate tabs with icons and counts, and groups have their own violet colour on their tiles, the Group button and group chats.

### 🗣️ Members talk as much as they should (Roleplay 4.37.0)
A card with no talkativeness of its own, or SillyTavern's usual 0.5, gets a guess from its personality: shy and quiet characters speak less, chatty and loud ones more (works for English, Ukrainian and Russian cards). The group editor shows each member's value and whether it is the card's or a guess.

### 🌍 Prose language is a pick (Roleplay 4.37.0)
FRANKENX's **Prose language** is a choice of the 14 interface languages plus **Same as my messages**. Old answers keep working; an update turns the old text field into the pick.

### 🧙 Molfar checks the UI it builds
When Molfar builds or changes an app or a plugin, a new built-in skill makes it check that a user can find, understand and use the new controls, on desktop and on a phone. Molfar's card skills now write new cards into the Profile tab's fields.

## Other changes
- The Roleplay app's chats carry a `pinned` flag on their meta.
- Scene generation leaves muted members out and shortens each card as the group grows, so small models keep up.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.38.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. They work on any Molfar Vertep 1.0.0.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.2".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.2`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.2-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.2-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.2` or `:latest`.
