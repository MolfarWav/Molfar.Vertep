# 🧬 Molfar Vertep 1.8.0

The world now builds itself from the story, and the Dashboard is rebuilt around what matters in play. This release is Roleplay 4.78.0 as an app update; the engine itself only changes its version. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then open Updates and press Update on Roleplay (the first open after the update does one full app build).

## 🌍 The world builds from the story (#176)
- **The World DM is on in every chat** at the Chronicler level and writes as the story goes: places, the rooms inside them, things and who holds them. A chat that never had a DM switches itself on at its first reply and starts near the end of the story, not at its first message; an assistant-only card stays off; the switch in World > Overview turns it off for good. It costs one model call every 5 replies and one when the story changes place, on the Dashboard sensor's model by default.
- **Places, things, outfit.** A place can be a spot inside a bigger one (a living room in a flat); a thing is held by someone or lies in a place and moves when the story moves it. A short note "World · here" goes with every reply (no extra model call): where the scene is, what lies there, what the people in it wear and hold, so clothes and things stop drifting. **Who keeps clothes and what is in the hands** is a per-chat choice: the Dashboard sensor (default, no extra tokens) or the DM (lists of worn, carried and nearby things). Places and things are always the DM's.
- **Settings with bounds:** the size of a record, the number of changes per run, a separate cadence for records, and a run when the story changes place. The settings panel is a list with an (i) popover for each hint, folded into groups.
- **World book, Places, backgrounds.** The Records tab is the **World book** and opens at once (a question first only for a world built by Start). A **Places** tab shows places, their rooms, visits and things; a place can be bound to a background that the scene in that place shows, or a picture can be generated for it from its names. A saved world shows its own World book and Places too.

## 🧭 The Dashboard, rebuilt
- **A wider side Dashboard** (340 px, drag the left edge to change it) with tabs **Person, Here, Quests, Notes** (the phone sheet adds History), each in its own colour, with a number in a circle for what changed since your last reply (red when something fell) and a "Since your last reply" row. The animated weather sits next to the clock.
- **New cards:** Attitude with the radar and the five stats side by side, Mood and state, Outfit and things, Here and Things. Negative values are red. Quests show their kind (main, side, everyday) in colour.
- **The open Dashboard** gets Here and Outfit and things, a World button that opens this chat's World, and a full-screen switch.

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart, then Update on Roleplay in Updates. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
