# 🧬 Molfar Vertep 1.9.6

The memory finds old things when you ask in other words, the history leaves room for every kind of memory on a small context window, and there is an optional hidden plan for a narrator. This is **Roleplay 4.85.0, an app update**; the engine itself has no code changes in this release. **Order:** get this engine release and restart, then open Updates and press Update on Roleplay.

## 🔎 The memory finds a fact when you ask in different words
- When a scene is written into the story record, the model now also adds a few **search words** to each new fact and chapter: synonyms of its main nouns, verbs and places, and the story's own language when the record is in English. The memory reads them when it looks for what to remind the model of. The model never sees them.
- Why: the record found things by their words only. A question like "where is the pendant?" did not find "Bram hides the silver locket". In a test chat of 5000 messages with every word of the question changed, the answer reached the prompt in **50% of cases with search words written by a paid model, 8% with a small local 9B model, and 0% without them**. It is a real but partial fix: a connected **embeddings model** (Settings, Memory) still finds more (85% in the same test with a simulated decent one; a real model was not measured), and a local embedder is being looked into in issue #216.
- On by default; the switch is in the Library, Litopys settings, "Facts and meaning". It costs about 20 tokens per fact in the call that writes the scene. **Old facts have none until you press "Rebuild from scratch"** on the record.
- A small price: with the exact words of a fact the match got a little less sharp in the test (the best-case score went from 0.99 to 0.93-0.96 at 5000 messages), because synonyms can match other facts too.

## 📏 The history leaves room for all the memory
- The room kept free for what the plugins add to a request counted only the Dashboard notes, the Litopys insert and the backstory link. It missed the character's long memory and habits, persona and world memory, recollections, "who knows what", the Dashboard's said lines, the World notes, event and dice, and the agents' hints. On a small context window they could push the prompt past the limit while nothing was trimmed.
- Now each block is counted at its own ceiling, and only when your chat can have it: a card with no memory, a switch turned off for the chat, a 1:1 chat with no narrator list count nothing. A chat with a lot of memory on a small window keeps a little less history; a plain chat is unchanged except for 120 tokens for the said lines.

## 🕵️ An optional hidden plan for a narrator (off by default)
- When one reply voices several characters and the "who knows what" list is on, the narrator can be asked to open with a short **hidden plan**: one line per acting character, with what that character may use. The plan is cut from the reply before it is shown or saved, with any preset. The switch is in the Library, Litopys settings, "Recollections": "Plan who knows what first, for a narrator".
- It costs about 150 tokens per reply. In a test of 24 narrator replies per model it cut secret leaks on a small local model from 6 to 2 on top of the list (17 without the list), and made no difference on the two paid models, which leak little anyway. It is off because the gain over the list alone is not proven.

## 🧠 Also in Roleplay 4.85.0 (from the memory-injection work)
- Litopys now says in which language to answer: its notes are in English while a story may be Ukrainian, and only the Dashboard block said so before.
- Recollections no longer contradict the story record, and the **newest recollection sits next to your message**; the recollections have their own budget (Litopys settings, "Recollections budget", 100 to 1500 tokens, default 300).
- Memory now says it **shapes how characters act**, not only that it must not be contradicted (an edited Litopys prompt stays yours).
- The Soul's **temperament** reaches the story model: a character's Dashboard block gets a "Temperament:" line.

## 🧪 For developers
- A recall bench (`test/rp-recall-mrr-bench.test.ts`) asks paraphrased questions about old facts and chapters at 500, 2000 and 5000 messages; the leak bench has a narrator condition with the plan.
