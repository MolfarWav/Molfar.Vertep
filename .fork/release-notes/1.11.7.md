# 🧠 Molfar Vertep 1.11.7

No engine code changed. **Roleplay 4.98.0 arrives as an app update**: a memory fact can be known to a whole group, a line under the newest reply says what the Dashboard recorded, and a chat that continues earlier chats can bring back word-for-word passages of them.

## 🏰 A fact known to a whole group
- A memory fact can now be known to a whole group, for example a guild, not only to named people. In the fact's **Known to** type `group: Guild of Ravens`; every member who is in the scene knows it, the others do not.
- Who belongs: a card with the tag `group: Guild of Ravens` (or `faction: Guild of Ravens`; other tags are not groups), or the **Members** field of a Group or Place record in the World book (Records tab; the World DM does not fill it).
- The Library shows the group as "Guild of ravens (group)". The model that writes the chapters is told the groups of the people in the scene and may use one for news the whole group learned; a made-up group is dropped. A snapshot book copies the members' names when you save it.
- No extra model call.

## 🧾 What changed, under the newest reply
- A muted line under the newest reply says what the Dashboard recorded for it: "Changed: Ada · trust fell, mood calm → tense, +2". Tap it to unfold the same chips as the Dashboard's "Since your last reply" row; a chip opens that Dashboard tab (on a phone, its bottom sheet).
- Only under the newest reply, and only once the Dashboard has updated for it. Hide it in Settings > Chat Behavior > "Show what changed under the newest reply".

## 🤝 Ties between characters reach the prompt
- The Dashboard already noted how two characters (not you) stand to each other, a one-word role and warmth ("rival, cold"), and never sent it. Now up to four ties ride in the Dashboard part, only for people in the scene: a character's own reply gets the ties it is part of, a narrator gets the ties of the people the turn is about first. About 20 to 60 tokens.

## 📜 Word-for-word memories of earlier chats
- A chapter is a retelling, so a small detail of an earlier chat (a cloak's colour, an exact promise) can be lost. In Library > Litopys settings > "In the prompt", switch on **Word-for-word memories of earlier chats** (off by default, 150 to 1200 tokens, 400 by default).
- A chat that continues others can then carry one or two passages of the ORIGINAL messages of any chat of the chain, not only the three nearest, when what is being said shares several rare words with them. They sit above the facts and say that the facts and later events win. A character only gets a passage of a scene it was in.
- The Memory view and the Fired panel list the passages of the newest reply; the Canvas Prompt board has a switch for the part. No model call.

## 📚 Docs
- The Agents guide page lists the four run lanes (before the reply, beside it, after it, by hand), not only "after every reply".
- The architecture notes and the README describe the memory features that are built, not the plan they began as.

## Known and left
- Word-for-word recall is words only: vectors do not take part yet. It was tuned on synthetic chats (a planted detail is found, ordinary text almost never brings a passage); nothing was run on a real long chat, and a short chat (under about 40 passages) returns nothing because there is too little evidence.
- Not checked with a real model: a chapter worker writing a group for news the whole group learned.
- The leak check does not look at facts told to a group yet. A group that exists only in the old chat's World records is unknown in a chat that continues it.
- The guide translations of the touched pages are stale (the reader shows English until they are refreshed).

## How to get it
From source: `git pull && bun install && (cd client-agent && bun install) && bun run build:client`, then restart, then Update on Roleplay in Updates. `dev` only updates the engine, not the Roleplay app. Downloads and the Android APK are on this release page.
