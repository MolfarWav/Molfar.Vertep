# 🧭 Molfar Vertep 1.2.5

Roleplay gets a World DM: a game master for each chat that remembers the world and, if you want it, moves it.

### 🌍 World DM
Open a chat's menu → **World DM** and pick what it does. It is off until you pick a level, and each chat has its own.
- **Chronicler** writes down the people, places, groups and facts the story meets, and what became of them. The records go into the chat's own lorebook, so the story model remembers them when they come up again.
- **Living world** adds fronts: factions and threats with a goal and a clock that move while you are elsewhere. News of them may reach your characters as a rumour; nothing is forced.
- **Director** brings hooks, complications and visitors into the scene. The next reply must use them.
- **Antagonist** brings pressure and consequences, aimed at what your characters care about (the Dashboard's threads).
- **Total chaos** lets anything happen, whatever the genre.

An event rides one reply. If the reply ignores it, the DM sends it once more, then lets it go.

### ⏱️ When it runs
Every N replies (5 by default), when a scene stands still too long, right after a time skip, or when you press **World turn**. With the Dashboard on, it knows the scene's direction: a scene at home may stand still longer before the DM steps in, one on the road less, and a fight never counts as standing still. New events also wait out a fight below Antagonist. An explicit scene freezes it completely, chaos included. It can use its own model, a small cheap one is enough, and its events have a switch on the Canvas prompt board.

### 👁️ The world stays the DM's, unless you want to look
The chat's world book is hidden from the Lorebooks library. **Look at the world** shows its records behind a spoiler warning; the DM's plans (fronts and events) stay hidden. Branching a chat copies its world; deleting the chat deletes it.

### 🗣️ Two languages in one chat
If you write in one language and the story replies in another, the DM writes the records in the story's language and adds your spelling of each name, so the lorebook finds them either way.

## Other changes
- **Roleplay 4.53.0** (an app update, Apps → Updates): the World DM; the Dashboard names the scene's direction (a new part of its prompt, "Direction"); the app is called Vertep in the shell (its id and data stay `roleplay`).
