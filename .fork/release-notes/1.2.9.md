# 🔍 Molfar Vertep 1.2.9

One new button: see in ten seconds whether a model's provider caches your prompt.

### 🔍 Check the cache
A cached prompt is cheaper and faster, but the chat cannot tell you why a reply showed nothing in the cache: the provider may not cache at all, may not report it, or may have dropped the cache while you were reading.

**Settings → Connections and models**, then the sliders next to a model: the **Prompt cache** row has a **Check the cache** button. It sends a prompt of about 2,500 tokens two or three times, one call after the other, with an answer of a few tokens, and tells you:
- **Caches**: how many tokens of the prompt came from the cache;
- **No cache**: the provider does not cache this model, or does not report it;
- **No token counts**: the provider reports none, so nothing can be checked.

The time of the calls is shown under it. The calls run in turn on purpose: a provider writes its cache while it reads the first call, so two calls at once would both miss. A miss gets one more try, because some providers spread calls over several servers and hit only on the next one.

What we saw on NanoGPT: Gemma 4 31B caches (2272 of 2298 tokens) and keeps it for a few minutes; GLM 5.3 caches only some of the time. How long a cache lives is the provider's choice; a reply that takes minutes plus your reading time can outlast it.

## Other changes
- The button is for the shell only: an app cannot spend your tokens through it.
- pi stays at 1.2.0: 1.3.0 is younger than the 7 days a new pi version waits.
