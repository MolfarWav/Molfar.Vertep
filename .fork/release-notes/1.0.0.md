# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 1.0.0

Group chats. Several characters in one scene, with a card of their own, a narrator that voices them all, and each one's lines in its own colour. They come with the Roleplay app 4.35.0; the engine itself is the same as 0.9.11.

### 👥 Groups get a home of their own
Characters now has a **Characters | Groups** switch at the top, so groups no longer get lost among single cards. Each group shows as a collage of its members' portraits. Deleting a group works now and takes its chats with it; the members stay. Before, a group looked like a broken character card that came back after a reload.

### 🛠️ A group editor
Open a group to edit it, in six tabs:
- **Overview:** name, picture (or the members' collage), tags, a note for yourself.
- **Members:** order, mute, and per member, in this group only: talkativeness, which opening message it brings (its group greeting, one of its greetings, or none) and its dialogue colour.
- **Scene:** a shared scenario that replaces each member's own, and the group's own openings (the first starts a new chat, the rest are swipes).
- **Turns:** who answers and how.
- **Lore:** the group's own lorebooks, and whether members' books count (all, only the speaker's, or none). Muted members' books stay out.
- **Chats:** the group's chats, and a new chat button.

"Group settings" in a group chat's member bar opens it too, and converting a chat into a group offers "Edit group".

### 🎙️ The narrator mode
New groups start in the narrator mode: one reply voices every member who is not muted, with all their cards in the prompt, so the scene moves in one model call per turn. Your existing groups keep "each in turn", where one member writes each message; switch in the Turns tab.

### 🎨 Each member speaks in its own colour
In group chats, each member's dialogue takes its colour: the one set in the group, else the card's, else one from a palette. In a narrator reply, a quote takes the colour of the member named first in its line. The colours can be turned off in the Turns tab.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- Character and persona profiles: "Fill empty" scrolls its proposal into view. It opened at the top of the card, out of sight of the section you pressed it in.

## Other changes
- Known gaps, coming in the next updates: scene generation for a group, the group part of the Chats page, a type filter in Characters. The palette's yellow is pale on light themes.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Group chats come with the Roleplay app 4.35.0:** open the launcher and update Roleplay. It works on Molfar Vertep 0.9.7 and later, so this engine update is optional for it.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.0.0".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.0.0`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.0-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.0.0-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.0.0` or `:latest`.
