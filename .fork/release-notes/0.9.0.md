# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.9.0

Long roleplays remember. Each chat now keeps one story memory, Litopys: chapters, facts and arcs that ride every reply while the old messages leave the prompt, and a Library where you can read and fix all of it. Your apps, chats and settings are kept; the old Memory moves into Litopys by itself.

## ✨ What's new

### 📜 One story memory per chat (Roleplay 4.25.0, through app updates)
Open **Updates** in the top bar to install Roleplay 4.25.0. Litopys cuts the story into scenes and, in the background, writes one chapter for each finished scene: what changed (who won or lost, who was hurt, what was promised, who learned what), not a retelling. With each chapter come the facts worth keeping: who a fact is about, who knows it, whether it is an event, a trait, a lasting change (a lost arm, a new title), a relation, a world fact or a plan, and how much it matters. A scene gets its chapter as soon as the next one begins. The memory model is your choice in the Litopys settings; empty means the chat's own model.

### 🧠 Long chats stay inside the model's window
Messages Litopys already holds leave the prompt. The newest ones (20 by default) always go word for word, and in place of the rest each reply carries one Litopys block within a token budget (800 by default): your pinned facts first, then the traits and lasting changes of the characters in the scene, the last chapter that left the prompt, and then the other facts and chapters that match what is happening now. A character only gets the facts they know. In the chat, messages that left the prompt are dimmed and a divider marks where the word-for-word part starts. The history is now measured in tokens, with room kept for the reply and for the dashboard's and Litopys's inserts.

### 🧵 Arcs for very long stories
A 300-message chat holds about 30 chapters, far more than the budget. When the older chapters outgrow a threshold (twice the budget by default), Litopys offers to merge a run of neighbouring chapters into one short arc that rides the prompt in their place; the chapters themselves stay in the record. By default it asks first: **Merge**, **Not now** or **Always merge automatically**. How many newest chapters stay whole, the threshold, how many chapters go into an arc and how long it may be are all settings. When you edit a chapter inside an arc, the arc is merged again.

### 📚 The Library
The rail section that was Litopys is now the **Library**, with two views of every chat's record:
- **Overview**: the chapters as a timeline with their facts under them and arcs as bands over their chapters, the proposals waiting for you, and a card for each character with pinned facts, traits, lasting changes, relations and what they know about others. Click a portrait to set one for a name that has no card (a narrator card's characters, lorebook people).
- **Ledger**: tabs for chapters, facts, proposals and activity, with filters and search.

In both you can edit, pin (up to 5 per character), retire, restore and delete facts; edit, rewrite or delete chapters, and keep a scene out of the record for good; and accept or reject what Litopys proposes (it never rewrites your own wording, it proposes). A line shows what the worker is doing: writing a chapter, waiting its turn, failed and retrying, or stalled. A map shows your chats and the chats forked from them.

### 🔎 Back to the source
**Open the messages** on any chapter or fact shows the messages it came from, and the Ledger can search the original messages of a chat, never the summaries, because a summary can be wrong.

### 🔁 Rebuild a chat's memory
The chat's **Memory** item opens its record: how many messages left the prompt, what the last reply carried, and **Rebuild from scratch**. A rebuild writes a new record beside the one in use and swaps it in when it is done, so replies never lose their memory meanwhile; the facts you wrote, edited or pinned and the chapters you edited are kept.

### 🧲 Matching by meaning in one step
Settings has a new **Memory** tab. It says whether matching by meaning (embeddings, which apps' memory and lorebooks use) works and through what, and lets you choose where embeddings come from: Automatic, OpenRouter, or one of your custom connections. With OpenRouter it is one step: paste an OpenRouter key and it is saved as your OpenRouter connection, with `qwen/qwen3-embedding-4b` as the default model. Before, this needed a custom connection made by hand. It is optional: without it, memory matches by words.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- Litopys reads chapters that some models return in a flat shape, and a chapter that comes out longer than the messages it replaces is asked for again, shorter.
- Deleting a chat now deletes its memory too.
- A language stored by older Roleplay versions ("English") is read again instead of being replaced by the browser's language.

## Other changes
- The built-in Memory (its summary and facts panel) is gone: its data and Litopys 1.x facts move into Litopys by themselves, and the old memory file is removed after the move. Roleplay Settings has a Litopys section instead (model, recent messages, insert and budget, scene sizes, pin limit, arcs, prompts).
- Litopys, the dashboard's sensor and fast mode write their records in English whatever the story's language, so they hold up across models; names stay as the story spells them.
- Dashboard notes follow the scene, near-duplicates are not written, the sensor gets the newest and the most relevant notes, and important notes older than 30 turns move into Litopys facts.
- Embeddings through OpenRouter go only to OpenRouter's own embeddings address, and each vector records the model that made it (vectors of different models do not compare).

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.9.0". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.25.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.9.0`.
- **Downloads** below: `Chrysalis-0.9.0-<system>` for desktops (names kept so self-update keeps working) and `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.0-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. The APK is debug-signed: a later APK cannot update it in place, install it fresh and keep a backup.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.9.0` or `:latest`.
