# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.9.9

A small release about order. Each Roleplay tool now keeps its settings with it, every built-in prompt can go back to its default, and the dashboard button is easier to find. With Roleplay 4.34.0.

## ✨ What's new

### 🧭 Settings live with their tools (Roleplay 4.34.0)
- **Litopys** settings open from a gear in the **Library**: the on/off switch and the chapter and arc prompts. **Settings → Memory** now points there.
- **The dashboard's** own settings gain the note age and the soul, nudge and fast prompts. A dashboard that has not been built yet already shows its gear.
- The Litopys and dashboard panels in **Tools** are gone. Tools keeps Tool Calling only.

### ↩️ Every prompt restores to its default (Roleplay 4.34.0)
- Each Litopys and dashboard prompt has its own **Restore default**. Restoring one leaves the others as they are.
- Utility prompts in an editable preset copy get **Restore default** too. The stock presets stay read-only.

### 📊 The dashboard button, easier to find (Roleplay 4.34.0)
**Open dashboard** moved from the bottom of the strip to the top, in the accent colour.

## Other changes
- The project page has a download button and our Discord link at the top: [discord.gg/NMqSehTCTs](https://discord.gg/NMqSehTCTs).
- The README shows the card profile and Persona in "What's already inside".

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.9.9". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.34.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.9.9`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.9-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-0.9.9-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.9.9` or `:latest`.
