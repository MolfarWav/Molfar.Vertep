# 🧾 Molfar Vertep 1.3.4

Groups are easy to find now, and Roleplay stops reloading its whole window for no reason. The Groups section is Roleplay 4.64.0, which comes as an app update; the reload fix is in the engine.

### 👥 Groups have their own section
Groups used to hide behind a **Characters | Groups** switch inside Characters. Now they have their own place in the menu, right after **Characters**; on a phone it is in **More**. Characters lists only single characters and has a **New character** button; Groups lists your groups and has **New group**. Opening a group from a chat (edit the group, open a member) takes you to the right section. The icons tell them apart: one person for Characters, three for Groups. The guide's Groups tips and "Show me" point at the new section.

### 🔁 No more surprise reloads (#83)
At start and about once an hour, Roleplay could reload its whole window with "Reloaded: live editing could not patch a change in place", sometimes two or three times in a row. On Windows the file watcher also reports files that nobody wrote: indexers, antivirus, backup and sync tools touch their attributes or access times. One such event on `index.html` or `bun.lock` made the in-browser builder run a full build, and every open page reloaded. The engine now remembers each build source's size and modification time, and passes a change on only when one of them actually moved. A real edit, a new file, a deleted or renamed one still updates the open app as before.

## Get it
Run from source with `Molfar-Vertep.bat` (latest release tag), or download the archive for your system from the assets below. The Groups section arrives as an app update through the Updates panel.
