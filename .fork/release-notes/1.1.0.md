# 🔄 Molfar Vertep 1.1.0

Carry your games between devices: push on the desktop when you stop, pull on the phone and go on, and back. Roleplay 4.48.0 brings a second built-in preset, Molfar, and every preset option now says what it does. Preset blocks that are meant to stay hidden finally stay hidden, apps install from USB drives, and exporting Roleplay data no longer times out.

### 🔄 Sync between your devices
**Settings → Sync** (also **Sync devices** in the account menu). Pick where the sync lives: **Google Drive** (your own Drive; Molfar Vertep sees only the folder it makes there) or **a folder on this computer** (a USB stick, a Syncthing folder, Google Drive for desktop). Then **Push** when you stop playing on one device and **Pull** on the other before you go on. It works like git, by hand, never in the background: the first push sends everything, later ones only what changed, and deleted files are deleted on the other side too.

**What travels:** apps with their data and your settings (chats, cards with pictures, presets, lorebooks, everything apps keep); Molfar's chats and projects; pictures, sound and video (switch them off to save space; cards then travel without pictures); and, if you want, your API keys and MCP settings, locked with a passphrase that never leaves your devices. Type the same passphrase on every device.

**When both devices changed the same file**, a pull lists those files and you choose **Keep mine** or **Take the synced one** for each (or one choice for all). The version you do not keep is saved in the engine's `data/backups/sync-conflicts` folder for 30 days. Everything else pulls as usual. A push after the other device pushed asks you to pull first.

**Signing in with Google:** press **Sign in with Google**. A new app may show "Google hasn't verified this app": choose **Advanced → Go to Molfar Vertep**. On a phone, if the page does not change after you signed in, copy the address from the tab Google opened and paste it in. You can use your own Google client under **Advanced** instead of the built-in one.

### 🧙 Molfar, a second built-in preset (Roleplay 4.48.0)
**Molfar** now sits next to FRANKENX in Presets: a modular preset for long roleplay. Pick it for a chat and open **Choices** to set it up: who writes your character, point of view, tense, style, story focus and modifiers, world genre, pace, difficulty, reply length (Short ~250 words to Detailed ~1600), formatting, prose and dialogue rules, NSFW level, HTML panels, the story's language and an optional written plan for models without built-in thinking. Its hidden blocks (the plan, the inner voice, the focus log) are removed from the chat by the preset's own regex scripts. It is read-only: duplicate it to change its text. Its instructions take about 13-14k tokens: use a model with a context window of 32k or more.

### 💡 Every option says what it does (Roleplay 4.48.0)
In **Choices**, an option now carries a short line saying what it changes: under the option in a list, and for chips in a tooltip and a line under the group (tap a chip on a phone). Every option of FRANKENX and Molfar has one. In the preset editor you can write them for your own presets, and Marinara presets bring their descriptions along.

### 📏 How big a preset is (Roleplay 4.48.0)
The preset picker in a chat now shows about how many tokens each preset takes with this chat's choices (`≈13.6k tokens`), instead of a count of sections and the temperature.

### 🔎 Finding a chat on the Prompt board (Roleplay 4.48.0)
The chat picker on **Canvas → Prompt** has a search (by character or chat title), shows recent chats first, marks group chats, and tells two chats of the same character apart by title, last activity and length.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Blocks a preset hides showed in the chat** (#36). Regex scripts that change only what you see (hiding a plan, an inner voice, a tracker line) never ran inside the app: the browser blocked the helper they run in. They work now, in every app.
- **Installing an app from a USB drive** (#34). On a drive that records no file owner (big exFAT USB drives) or a folder another Windows account owns, Git refused the freshly downloaded app, so installing Roleplay failed even after the launcher trusted the Molfar Vertep folder. The engine now trusts only the folders it creates itself.
- **Export Roleplay data timed out** (#37, Roleplay 4.48.0). Settings → Data → Export failed on an ordinary library ("exceeded the maximum allowed time limit"). The engine now packs the zip: an 8 MB library exports in about a second.
- **"What the characters know about you was left out"** (#35, Roleplay 4.48.0). The dashboard's notice after a reply sounded as if the facts about you were gone. It now says how many notes did not fit that one reply (the least relevant ones) and that they stay in the notebook; it suggests a bigger limit only when even the pinned notes do not fit.
- Molfar's option labels showed raw `{{user}}` text (Roleplay 4.48.0).

## Other changes
- Plugins: `host.zip.build([{ name, text }])` returns a zip of those texts as base64, built natively, without a permission. The `app-authoring` skill mentions it.
- App frames allow workers from `blob:` URLs; such a worker inherits the frame's policy, so it still has no network.
- Roleplay: an update adds Molfar and its scripts when they are missing and fills option hints into the built-in FRANKENX and Molfar copies; a copy you made your own is left alone. What's new points at Presets.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **The Roleplay changes come with the app 4.48.0:** update Roleplay (the launcher, or the app's update button). The faster export and the hidden Molfar blocks need Molfar Vertep 1.1.0; the rest works on 1.0.0 and later.
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v1.1.0".
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v1.1.0`.
- **Downloads** below:
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.1.0-<system>` for desktops;
  - `Molfar-Vertep-1.1.0-android-arm64.apk` for Android 9 and newer. It installs over 0.9.1 and later and keeps your data.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v1.1.0` or `:latest`.
