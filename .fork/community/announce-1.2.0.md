# #announcements (English, under Discord's 2,000 characters)

🎨 **Molfar Vertep 1.2.0 + Roleplay 4.49.0 are out**

**Pictures from more places.** Image generation is no longer OpenRouter-only:
• **NanoGPT**: uses the NanoGPT key you already have, prices shown next to each model
• **Your own server** in the new **Settings → Images**: ComfyUI (ready workflow, or paste your own), AUTOMATIC1111 / Forge, or any OpenAI-compatible server
Their models show up in Roleplay's image picker on their own, and "avoid" becomes a real negative prompt on ComfyUI and Forge.

**Thousands of characters.** Roleplay used to stop opening at around 700 characters. Now it starts from a light list and loads a card in full only when you use it: **2,000 characters open in about 2 seconds**. The first start after the update builds that list in the background (about half a minute for 2,000). Big imports now go in batches, so they no longer stop halfway.
A one-click move of a whole SillyTavern library (characters, personas, chats) is next, and this release is the ground it needs.

**The Library tells you when it waits on you.** When the Library suggests pinning, merging or rewriting a fact, you get a count on the Library icon and on the chat, plus a notice where you can accept or reject it on the spot. Turn it off in Library settings.

**Fixed:** a Dashboard thread you closed no longer comes back open on the next turn, and resolved threads stay in the list as history.

Update: restart `Molfar-Vertep.bat`, or press **Update to 1.2.0** in the app.
Full notes: https://github.com/MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep/releases/tag/v1.2.0
Found a bug? #bug-reports, one bug per post 🐞
