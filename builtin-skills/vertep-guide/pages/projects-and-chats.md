# Projects and chats

There are two kinds of chats in Molfar Vertep, and they are easy to confuse. This page explains both and how projects fit in.

## Chats with Molfar

These are conversations with the agent about your apps, files and settings. They live on the agent page, in the sidebar. "New chat" starts one, and the search box finds old ones. A chat's menu has Rename, Move to project, Archive and Delete; archived chats are under Archived. You can also drag a chat onto a project. The sidebar folds to a thin strip, and on a phone it is a drawer.

Hover a message for its buttons: Copy, Refresh (asks again) on a reply, Edit on your own message. Edit and Refresh remove that message and everything after it, then run again from there. They do not undo changes already made to apps; use Undo for that (see [[molfar]]).

## Roleplay chats

These are your stories with characters. They live inside the Roleplay app, with its own list, branches, swipes and memory. They are separate from your chats with Molfar. How they work is in the Roleplay guide: [[roleplay:index]].

## Projects

A project groups chats with Molfar and gives all of them the same context. It has:

- **Instructions**: a text Molfar reads at the start of every chat in the project.
- **Memory** and **Skills**: kept for this project.
- **Files**: images and text files you drop in, which the chats can use. Molfar sees the list and reads what he needs.
- **Default model**: the model new chats in the project start on, or your own default.

A chat inside a project says "Project context attached".

There are two sorts:

- **App projects.** Every installed app is a project, marked APP. Its page also has Checkpoints.
- **Free projects.** Make one with the + beside Projects: enter a title and press Create, or use Import zip to bring in a project someone gave you. Use them for ideas, research or plugins that are not an app.

## Where to find a project

Select it in the sidebar. In a project chat, the header button "Show the project pane" opens a side pane next to the chat; its arrows button opens the full page with the tabs Overview, Instructions, Memory, Skills, Checkpoints (apps only) and Files. On a phone a Chat | Project switch does the same.

## How they relate

A project is a shelf of context for Molfar's chats. It is not a Roleplay story and does not hold Roleplay chats. Starting a chat in an app's project means Molfar knows that app's own notes and instructions. The words are explained in [[glossary]].
