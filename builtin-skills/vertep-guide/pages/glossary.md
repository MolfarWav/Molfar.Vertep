# Glossary

The words in Molfar Vertep that people most often mix up, one short entry each. The rest of the guide uses them exactly this way.

## The program

**Molfar Vertep** is the whole program: the thing you start on your computer and open in a browser. It holds your account, your apps, your model connections and Molfar.

**The shell** is the frame around everything: the start page, the tabs along the top, the account menu at the top right and the Settings dialog. It is not an app. When this guide says "Settings", it means the shell's Settings.

**The start page** ("Where to?") lists Molfar and your installed apps, and has the buttons Store, New app and Import app. A new tab (+) opens it.

## Apps and Molfar

**An app** is a self-contained program that runs inside Molfar Vertep, with its own screens and its own data. **Roleplay** is the main one. Each open app lives in its own tab. You get apps from the Store, from a git repository or from a backup file, or build your own with Molfar.

**Molfar** is the built-in AI agent. He changes your apps, character cards, lorebooks, themes and settings files when you ask. He is a helper, not an app you play with.

**The agent page** is Molfar's tab: his chats on the left, the conversation in the middle. Everything in his tab is about talking to Molfar.

**A chat with Molfar** is a conversation on the agent page. It is not a Roleplay chat. Roleplay chats, with your characters, live inside the Roleplay app.

**A project** groups chats with Molfar and gives all of them shared instructions, memory, skills and files. Every installed app is a project of its own, and you can make free projects too. It has nothing to do with a Roleplay story. See [[projects-and-chats]].

## Your files

**Your workspace** is the folder of files that belongs to your account: your apps with their data, Molfar's memory and skills, your notes and settings. Molfar edits files in it, always within limits you can see.

## Models

**A connection** is a saved link to a place that provides models: a provider with your API key, a subscription sign-in, a local server or your own address.

**A model** is the AI itself, the thing that writes the replies. One connection can bring hundreds of models. You pick the model in a chat. See [[models]].

## Extras

**A plugin** is a small piece of code added to an app, usually to give it a new background ability. Plugins list the permissions they ask for.

**A skill** is a short instruction that teaches Molfar one kind of job, for example how you like character cards written. He loads a skill only when a task needs it.

**Memory** is what Molfar keeps between chats: notes about you and your work. He proposes each entry and saves it only when you agree. Do not confuse it with story memory inside Roleplay, which belongs to the app.
