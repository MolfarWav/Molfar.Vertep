# Apps

How to find, add, update and remove apps. Apps are the things you actually use: Roleplay is the main one.

## The start page

A new tab (+) opens the start page, "Where to?". It lists Molfar and your installed apps. On a computer, click an app to see what is inside and press "Launch"; the details also have "Export app" and, for apps that have an update source, "Check for updates". On a phone a tap opens it at once and the (i) button shows details. The trash icon deletes an app after asking, and this removes its data permanently.

Every open app has its own tab. Tabs can be dragged into any order and come back after a refresh.

## While an app is open

The toolbar offers:

- "Split": Molfar on the left, the app live on the right.
- "Fullscreen": the app in its own browser tab, which you can install like an app.
- "Plugins": the app's plugins and their permissions; import more from git.
- "Rebuild": rebuilds the app right now.

On a phone these three sit in the account menu.

## The Store

"Store" on the start page lists apps from Molfar Vertep (marked "Official App") and from the community. Press "Install": you first see what the app bundles and what it can reach, and nothing runs until you confirm. Install only what you trust, because apps run real code on your computer. "Refresh" asks for new apps, and a badge counts those added since you last looked.

## New and imported apps

- "New app": give it a name (an author is optional) and you get a starter that Molfar builds out with you.
- "Import app": from a git repository or from a backup file made by "Export app". It is inspected first, and nothing runs until you have read what it bundles and confirmed.

## Updates

The download icon in the top bar gets a dot when something is waiting. It lists every app with an update source and has "Check now". "Update all" appears when two or more apps wait, and never includes Molfar Vertep itself.

Your own edits are merged into an update, and your data is kept. Where your edits overlap with the new version, nothing changes yet: you choose "Keep mine", "Take update" or "Ask Molfar to merge". An update that installs new packages or asks for new permissions waits for your OK.

## Where app settings are

An app's own settings are inside the app. The shell's Settings holds what all apps share: models, speech, images, memory, MCP servers. See [[settings]].

## If an app fails

When an app's build fails, it keeps its last good version, and its error bar offers "Ask Molfar", which sends him the problem in a new chat. See [[help-and-bugs]].
