# Help and bug reports

Where to find answers inside Molfar Vertep, and what to do when something is broken.

## Help

"Help" in the account menu (top right) is a searchable list of short answers. Each answer has a "Show me" button that takes you to the place on screen. The agent page has its own Help button for its screens: in the header on a computer, in the sidebar on a phone.

## Tours and What's new

A short tour starts the first time you open a screen. "What's new" appears after an update and points out new features. In Settings, General, Guide you can turn the tours off, play them again, or show What's new again.

## Ask Molfar

For a question about your own setup, ask Molfar in his tab. When an app fails, its error bar has an "Ask Molfar" button that sends him the problem in a new chat. See [[molfar]].

## Report a bug

"Report a bug" is in the account menu, and also at the bottom of the start page next to the Discord link. It works in steps:

1. Describe what happened: what you did just before and the exact words of any error.
2. Check the device and browser line. Adding your phone or computer model helps.
3. Take a screenshot. On a computer your browser asks which window or tab to capture; on a phone, take it yourself and attach it later.
4. Open "Open on GitHub" to get a pre-filled issue (a free GitHub account is needed), or "Copy for Discord" to paste the text into Discord.

Versions, the screen size and the latest errors go with the report. Nothing is sent from the form itself: you read it first and send it yourself.

## Ask Molfar first

The "Ask Molfar first" block in the same form hands your description to Molfar instead of GitHub. It opens a new chat and sends the text, with your device details, the latest errors and the app's name. They go only to Molfar and the model you connected.

He looks for the cause and tries a fix in your copy of the app, and you test it. If it works, a card "It works, send the fix" opens a form you can edit, and the fix goes to GitHub only when you submit it there. If it is still broken, "Still broken" sends that to Molfar. If the cause is in Molfar Vertep itself, he cannot fix it, and the card offers "Report it on GitHub".
