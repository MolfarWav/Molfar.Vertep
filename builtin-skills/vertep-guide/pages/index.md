# Molfar Vertep guide

Molfar Vertep is a program that runs on your own computer or phone and opens in your browser. It hosts apps, of which **Roleplay** is the main one: long chats with AI characters. You bring the AI model yourself (an API key, a subscription sign-in or a model on your machine). Inside lives **Molfar**, a built-in agent who changes the apps for you when you ask in plain words.

Your chats, characters and keys stay in files on your own disk. Nothing leaves the machine except the calls to the model you connected.

This guide is the overview: what each part is and how the parts fit together. Each app also has its own Help for its screens.
